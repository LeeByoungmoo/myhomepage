## B. Korean setting fingerprints (MEDIUM)
The story is nominally American (Jason, Sarah, 911) but the **built environment is a Korean apartment danji**:
| Text | Why it's off for US readers | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| "unit 802", "apartment complex entrance", "mechanical room in the apartment basement", the whole complex as a fortified mini-society | Korean high-rise danji culture; US apartments rarely work as closed compounds | Recast as a **gated condo complex / housing project**, use "apartment 8B" |
| "my mom back in my **hometown**" | 고향 calque | "my mom back home" |
| `'kimchi stew'` in a dead man's search history | Korean food in US office worker's life | "chili", "mac and cheese" |
| "**Director** Miller… I don't know how to boil water" | 부장님 → "Director" is a calque | "Mr. Miller, the regional VP" / "his old boss, Miller" |

| "Tch...", "Bwahaha!" | anime/webtoon onomatopoeia | "Christ.", "He roared with laughter." |
| "This son of a bitch really...!" | 이 새끼가 진짜…! — trailing-off + `!` is KR | "You son of a bitch!" |

## C. Sensitivity (HIGH for Western audience)
- **Dylan, a cross-dresser**, is described with "*'female intonation'*", "disastrous" made-up face, and another character mocks "that bastard Dylan wearing makeup". Western readers (and Reddit communities you plan to promote on) will read this through a trans/queer-representation lens.
- Not a reason to cut the character — the scene is actually sympathetic (he provides comfort/"emotional labor"). But: remove scare-quotes, avoid narrator-voice mockery, and give Dylan interiority/agency. Consider a sensitivity pass before Reddit promotion.
- The gender thesis ("emotional labor that women used to provide") is stated by the narrator in bold — **show, don't tell**; let the scene carry it.

## D. Prose (LOW)
- "Jason reached out out of habit" → "Out of habit, Jason reached over and silenced the alarm."
- "the doomsday clock had stopped forever" — mixed metaphor (the Doomsday Clock doesn't stop) → "every clock in the world seemed to stop."
- Over-intensifiers: "A perfect, absolute void of sound", "extreme chaos", "terrifying degree".
