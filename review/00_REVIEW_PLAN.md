## Review checklist (per novel)
- Symbols: Korean quotes 「」『』, ‘…’ misuse, `~`, `…` vs `...`, full-width chars, em-dash usage, `ㅋ`, emoji
- Formatting: markdown leftovers (`**`, `#`, `---`), episode headers, scene breaks
- Names: Korean-style names in US setting, inconsistent spelling, honorific leftovers (oppa, hyung, -ssi, sunbae)
- Dialogue: translationese ("Aigo", "Omo", overly formal speech, "Mr. Kim" patterns), unnatural idioms
- Culture: Korean-specific concepts unexplained (jeonse, hagwon, soju) in US setting
- Units/dates: metric vs imperial for US setting, KRW vs USD, date format
- Prose: repetition, over-explaining, tense shifts, AI-ish phrasing ("a testament to", "tapestry", "couldn't help but")
