# Step 5 — *Liquidation* (30 eps, ~40.5k words)

> Read: EP1 (lines 1–40), EP12 (lines 1–30) + scan data
> Overall: **The strongest English prose in the library.** Literary, satirical, confident voice (billionaires emerge from a 10-year bunker into a re-wilded Manhattan; capitalism re-forms and collapses). Needs formatting + a handful of K-isms only.

## A. Marketing mismatch (HIGH)
- Site: *"A sci-fi action thriller about a futuristic enforcer."* — **wrong**. There's no enforcer; it's a **dark social satire / survival** story (think *Succession* meets *Lord of the Flies*).
- Recommended synopsis: *"After ten years in a luxury doomsday bunker, the world's 100 richest people step out into a planet that no longer needs them. Their money is worthless. Their instincts are not."*
- Tags: `Satire, Survival, Sci-Fi` (drop "Thriller/enforcer").
- Cover (rain-soaked agent) does not match → overgrown Manhattan ruins + figures in bespoke hazmat suits.

## B. Formatting (HIGH)
- Same as *The Day*: line 1 = bare number, line 2 = title. (Here a blank line follows the title in some files → title shows as an un-styled paragraph.) → `## Episode N: Title`.
- 80 Unicode ellipses `…` (consistent within the novel — fine; just align site-wide).

## C. K-isms / translationese (MEDIUM)
| Text | Issue | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| "phytoncide fragrance" | 피톤치드 is a Korean wellness-marketing word; unknown to most EN readers | "pine-forest scent" |
| "the sheen of premium **ampoules**" | K-beauty term | "luxury serums" |
| "going to **blow out my lumbar disc**" | 허리 디스크 나간다 calque | "going to **throw out my back**" |
| "individual quarters scarcely **ten square meters**", "**two-meter**-thick", "3.1 meters by 3.1 meters" | Metric in a Manhattan/US setting | "barely a hundred square feet", "six-foot-thick", "ten feet by ten" |
| "fourth-generation heir… Preston Huntington **III**" | III = third → "fourth-generation" contradicts | make him "IV" or "third-generation" |
| "**Wall Street's legendary hedge fund manager**, muttered as he wrinkled his brow" | fine, but frequent appositive-intro pattern for every character (KR intro style) | vary introductions |

## D. Prose (LOW)
- **"grotesque/grotesquely"** appears 3× in EP1 alone and 2× in EP12 → site-wide crutch word (count in Step 9).
- Occasional over-explanation of the satire ("This miniature capitalism had collided head-on with a perfect contradiction. In the civilized era, capitalism had functioned because…") — trust the reader; cut ~20% of the lecture paragraphs.

## E. Names
- **Julian "Jules" Cross** (also Julian in *Cell Division* & *Echoes*), **Chloe** (in 5 novels), **David** (also Pressure's father), **Arthur** (also *Cell Division*). → rename here or elsewhere (Step 9).
