The Prisoner on the Chair
Although the cracking sounds of breaking bones and tearing flesh filled the lobby, a bizarre silence like a vacuum flowed through the space around Jason and Carter.

As soon as he met Jason's chilling eyes, Carter panted like a beast and backed away. His muscular frame, which once looked so massive, had shrunk pitifully and was trembling like a child receiving punishment.

"What... what guilt are you talking about! I fed my family! I crawled on the gym floor to make money for them!"

Carter screamed as if having a seizure. But Jason unswervingly grabbed Carter by the collar and forcefully dragged him toward the broken armchair.

"The patriarch who fed his family? The predator who protected this camp?" Jason threw Carter onto the chair. "You must have felt like you would go crazy if you didn't brainwash yourself like that. Because you couldn't handle what you did that morning."

"Shut up! What do you know about my wife and daughter!"

"I know everything." Jason whispered low and dryly into Carter's ear. "When all the women in the world quietly passed away from unknown cardiac arrest... Only your wife and daughter died in terrible terror."

Carter's eyes widened as if they would tear. His lips turned blue and began to tremble violently. Jason's voice was mercilessly ripping off the seal of the memory Carter had buried in the deepest, darkest place in his mind, trying so hard to ignore.

"The night before the event broke out. You went home drunk because your business failed. And in a fit of anger, you assaulted your wife. You even threw your seven-year-old daughter who was trying to stop you." Jason's voice was as numb as a judge's ruling.

"What did you do when your wife ran into the master bedroom holding your daughter? Because of your petty pride that they ignored you, you locked the door from the outside. Saying you wouldn't give them a single drop of water until they reflected on their actions."

"A, aaah... Stop, stop it!" Carter screamed, covering both ears. But Jason forcefully ripped Carter's hands away and shoved the truth in.

"The next morning, when an unknown phenomenon struck women all over the world. Your wife and daughter knocked on the firmly locked bedroom door in the agony of suffocating. They begged you to save them, to please open the door, Daddy. You were sitting on the living room sofa, listening to all those sounds. But because you felt slighted, because of that disgusting arrogance to prove you were the 'king' of the house, you didn't open the door until the very end."

Carter's pupils completely lost their focus. Covered in tears and snot, a bizarre metallic sound, like a beast being slaughtered, leaked from his throat.

"You opened the door hours after the knocking stopped. Seeing the corpses of your wife and daughter, stiffened and hanging onto the doorknob with all their fingernails torn out, you realized. That you were not a ruler, but a terrible murderer who starved and neglected his beloved family to death."

Jason slowly raised his head and pointed to the barricade in the chaotic lobby.

"Look, Carter." Jason's cold finger swept across the entire lobby. "That massive barricade firmly blocking the entrance. The rules that controlled people by taking their food. The violence of locking those who rebelled in the basement. Do you know what all this is?"

Carter, with unfocused eyes, looked back and forth between the broken iron fence and the bloodless ghosts.

"This massive apartment camp is not a fortress for survivors. It is a set for your massive guilt, an exact magnification of 'the living room that morning' when you locked the bedroom door and killed your family!" Jason's shout struck down like lightning. "You weren't playing boss here. To cover up the hellish guilt that you failed to protect your family—no, that you killed them with your own hands... You eternally tied yourself to this prison called an armchair. Repeating the futile play that 'I am a strong predator who controls and protects people'!"

Carter rolled off the chair. He slammed his head against the cement floor and wailed like a beast.

"I didn't kill them! It was a disease! All the women in the world died! I didn't do it... I didn't kill them...!" Carter pleaded, reaching his bloodstained hands toward the empty air.

"Yes, all the women in the world died. But in their final moments, your wife and daughter saw 'the hell that was you', not the fear of death. And that morning, you too died of shock in that living room, your heart stopping."

Jason slowly picked up the pistol Carter had dropped. The true face of Carter, stripped of the dictator's mask, was miserable. He was not a reigning tyrant, but the most wretched and pitiful prisoner in this camp, trapped in the most terrible self-hatred, unable to take a single step forward, wandering in a 100-year terminal purgatory.

"Get up, Carter." Jason said coldly, pulling the slide of the pistol. "It's time to end this petty play you wanted to protect so badly."

Carter raised his tear-stained face and looked up at Jason. The black muzzle of the pistol was slowly rising, aiming not at Carter, but at Jason's own punctured chest.

On the stage where the sins of the fake ruler had been exposed in their entirety, the final catharsis that would completely collapse even the physical laws of the world was being prepared.
