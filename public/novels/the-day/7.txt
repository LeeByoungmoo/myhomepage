The Monetization of Memory and Taboo Words
In the late dawn after food rations ended, the old boiler room on the dark second basement floor of the apartment was the camp's only black market where secret transactions took place.

But what was exchanged here were not survival necessities like canned tuna or antibiotics. The man sitting under the lantern light in the corner of the boiler room was a former perfumer who used to work on the first floor of a department store before the event. In front of him were scattered various cheap air fresheners, industrial alcohol, and unidentified herbal powders.

"Make this smell exactly like the lotion my wife used. It smelled kind of like... peaches."

A scrawny man held out all his food ration tickets with trembling hands. It was a lifeline meant to last him a week.

The perfumer routinely took the ration tickets and mixed liquids in a beaker, creating a bizarre fragrance. The man, receiving the liquid in a small glass bottle, buried his nose in it and inhaled deeply.

"Hrk... Ahh..." Thick tears poured from the man's eyes. It was a crude imitation reeking of alcohol, but in that smell, the man was desperately trying to reassemble the body odor, the scent of flesh, the warmth of his wife that had evaporated forever. He sprawled on the floor, hugging the glass bottle, wailing like a beast.

It had only been a few months since women disappeared. In the minds of the men, the faces, voices, and scents of their wives and lovers were scattering like fog. Unable to endure the horrific sense of loss and the evaporation of information, men tried to buy 'hallucinations of them' even if it meant offering up their survival supplies.

A former artist who could sketch a wife's smiling face hauntingly identical just from a description. A man who perfectly mimicked the soft voice and intonation unique to women.

The most expensive and powerful currency traded in this black market was not food to fill rotting meat, but 'fragments of memory' imitating the fleeting traces of absent women. It was a sad drug party of ghosts giving up physical hunger to soothe spiritual starvation.

Jason left the bizarre black market scene behind and came up to the first-floor lobby. That was when it happened. A sharp shout erupted among the men standing night guard in front of the barricade.

"Hey, you retard! Where did you leave your eyes! Is that what your mom taught you?"

That single sentence, blurted out by a young man in his twenties during a petty argument over guard rotation, instantly froze the lobby, filled with dozens of men, into a perfect vacuum.

Jason's steps, moving to break up the fight, also stopped in place. A vulgar insult regarding one's mother was common in the past. But now that women were gone forever, the word **'mother'** had become a domain of absolute sanctity that human's dirty mouths dared not utter lightly.

The absolute being who conceived and cared for life. The word had now become a god of legend that could never be met or touched again.

Carter, who had been half-asleep in the armchair, flashed his eyes open. His eyes gleamed chillingly, like a fanatic who had discovered a heretic.

"Grab him." The moment Carter's short order fell, Mike and the mob wearing red armbands rushed in, twisted the young man's limbs, and pinned him to the floor.

"N-No! It was a mistake... it came out unconsciously! Boss, please spare me!" The young man, realizing his mistake, turned deathly pale and struggled, but none of the men surrounding him felt any sympathy. Rather, their eyes burned fiercely, as if placing a demon who had blasphemed divinity at the stake.

Carter slowly stood up and approached the young man. "You insult the Creator, the Mother, with that dirty snout?"

"I was wrong! Please...!"

"Mother, daughter, wife. These sacred names are now words beasts like us have no right to utter. Your tongue has lost the right to speak human words."

When Carter nodded, Mike mercilessly grabbed the young man's jaw and forced his mouth open. A gleaming hunting knife reflected the fluorescent light.

A desperate scream tore through the lobby. A bloody chunk of meat fell onto the cement floor with a thud. The young man collapsed, clutching his mouth, foaming and convulsing.

Jason stood among the bloodshot crowd, silently watching the cruel punishment. That blind deification that would cut out the tongue of their own kind just for insulting the word 'mother'. It wasn't because they were repenting for their past, when they placed women at the bottom of violence and hierarchy. By blindly worshiping what they could not reach, it was merely a self-defense mechanism trying to somehow package the horrifying truth that they were abandoned beings.

But Jason's gaze remained on the young man's severed tongue rolling on the floor. Even though the tongue was cut off, the blood flowing from the young man's mouth did not spray everywhere. Like black waste oil leaking from an old machine, it was spreading very thickly and heavily onto the cement floor. Even the young man's convulsions seemed not out of pain, but closer to a bizarre nervous reflex created by the compulsion that he *should* feel pain.

Jason took a step back into the darkness. A world where language is castrated, memories are traded, and myth justifies violence. The men in this camp, shedding their shells of humanity one by one, were already taking irreversible steps toward the deepest part of hell.
