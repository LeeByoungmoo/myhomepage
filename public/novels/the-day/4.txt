Castrated Patriarchs and Transparent Slaves
Leaving the mart, Jason cut across the underground parking lot of his apartment complex. The dark parking lot was once a massive showcase displaying the pride of successful males. Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Porsche. The gleaming metal idols they had won even if it meant taking out massive loans or sacrificing weekends with their families for overtime.

But now, a thick layer of cement dust covered those hundred-thousand-dollar hunks of scrap metal like a shroud. The gasoline had already been looted by other men, and the luxury cars with dead batteries were nothing more than bulky steel coffins.

Jason stopped in front of a Mercedes S-Class with a familiar license plate. Director Miller, who had been crying while hugging canned goods on the mart floor, was now sitting in the driver's seat of his car. In a car that wouldn't even start, he gripped the steering wheel tightly, staring into thin air with bloodshot eyes.

"Honey... I bought this car for you to ride in. The ride is nice, isn't it?"

Director Miller's lips moved. The passenger seat was empty, but he was desperately muttering as if talking to someone.

"I put the luxury apartment under joint ownership for you. To pay for our daughter's study abroad, I bowed my head to those young punks and gave them my liver and gallbladder... I worked myself to the bone..."

Jason quietly watched the tragic collapse from outside the window. Immediately after women disappeared, the first to go insane in society were, ironically, the most 'patriarchal' men. They had accumulated social status and wealth, firmly believing themselves to be the 'rulers' and 'protectors' of their families.

But there was a cruel truth that Jason also realized only after Sarah vanished. Traditional masculinity—the petty pride of working oneself to the bone to earn money and provide for the family—was a mirage that could only exist when there was an 'other who looks up to and depends on you (wife and children)'.

Once the object of protection disappeared, the wealth and status built over a lifetime scattered into the void. Director Miller was not the king of his family. He was merely a 'transparent slave' who had ground up his time and emotions for the safety of his family inside the giant hamster wheel of capitalism. What was left for a slave who lost his master was the terrifying, vacuum-like emptiness of realizing the purpose of a lifetime of labor had evaporated.

"Why did you all leave me behind! I did everything for you! I gave you everything!"

Inside the car, Director Miller began screaming like he was having a seizure, smashing his own head against the steering wheel. *Bang! Bang! Bang!* A dull, cracking sound echoed through the glass. Jason could hear Miller's forehead skin tearing and bones hitting, but a bizarre scene caught Jason's eye.

Even though Director Miller was smashing his head so hard, there were no red blood droplets splattering on the airbag. Miller's forehead had split open like a parched rice paddy in a drought, but the inside was as dry as a withered sponge. Unable to bleed, Director Miller seemed not even to feel pain; dictated solely by emotional attachment and momentum, he mechanically bashed his head against the wheel.

Jason turned his gaze to the Mercedes' side mirror. Jason's own reflection was visible in the dust-covered mirror. No, more precisely, the reflection that *should have been* there was eerily blurry. Even standing under the dim emergency light of the parking lot, Jason's shadow stretching to the floor was so faint it was barely recognizable.

*We haven't been castrated.*

Jason stared at his own hollow eyes in the side mirror and thought. Once the mirror (women) that granted them identity as husbands, fathers, and men shattered, even the physical forms of the men that existed only as reflections in that mirror were slowly evaporating physically. The bodies that had lost their purpose stopped producing blood, and the skin unreached by the gaze of the other lost its body heat.

*Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!* The car shook grotesquely from Director Miller's manic flailing. Unable to watch the miserable play of the transparent slave any longer, Jason turned away.

As Jason exited the underground parking lot, a roar like the growl of a beast began to echo behind him. The era of the old males dying in sorrow and emptiness was setting, and the savage young males, fueled solely by hunger and rage to loot others, were gathering at the apartment complex dragging steel pipes.

Above the concrete jungle thick with the smoke of despair, a bloody night was arriving where new rulers would reign.
