The Creator in the Mechanical Room
Deepest in the apartment's underground parking lot, two men wearing red armbands stood guard in shifts in front of the mechanical room where massive pipes were tangled together.

It was the place where 'hope', another weapon besides force that Carter used to control people in this camp, was cultivated. On Carter's orders, Jason carried a portion of the food rations and entered the mechanical room.

As soon as he opened the heavy iron door, a sourly rotting chemical smell and the stench of rotten meat hit him. The unventilated basement had been remodeled like a bizarre operating room, relying on a few incandescent bulbs.

"Professor, I brought the food."

In the middle of the dim mechanical room, a former biotechnology professor was hunched over, fiddling with pipettes and petri dishes. His hollow eyes gleamed with madness, and his unwashed hair, neglected for days, was tangled like a bird's nest.

"Jason, you're here? Come here. Look at this. I finally found the answer!"

Professor Harrison didn't even glance at the canned goods Jason set down; he pulled Jason's sleeve with a voice full of ecstasy.

The reason Carter didn't kill Professor Harrison and gave him special treatment in a world without women was clear. In a closed ecosystem where only a single sex, male, remained, the illusion that 'we can conceive life ourselves' meant eternal power. Professor Harrison was pathologically obsessed with extreme asexual reproduction—inducing reproduction using only male somatic cells, namely **'Parthenogenesis'**.

"The combination of germ cells like sperm and eggs is no longer necessary. We apply chemical stimulation and electric shocks to the skin cells of our men, which have been dedifferentiated into stem cells, to induce embryonic division! Applying the survival mechanism that occurs in bees or lizard populations when females disappear to humans. We can evolve without women!"

Science, the most rational academic discipline, had degenerated into the most blind religion in the face of the massive fear of human extinction. Professor Harrison firmly believed that this bizarre research, going against the fundamental providence of life, would succeed, and he guarded the front of the microscope without sleeping.

Carter broadcasted this 'asexual reproduction research' through a megaphone every morning. "Professor Harrison's research has entered the next phase. Soon, we will birth a new humanity with our own power!" The men in the camp, despite being treated like beasts, did not start a riot. The arrogant yet desperate hope that they could reproduce and perpetuate without women. Carter was begging for the men's obedience with an intangible scientific miracle as collateral.

"Come here and look! The embryonic cell has finally started dividing into two! It's life, Jason. We are conceiving life ourselves!"

Pressing Jason's shoulder with brutal strength, Professor Harrison shoved Jason's face in front of the microscope lens.

Jason reluctantly brought his eye to the lens. But at that moment, a horrifying chill ran down Jason's spine.

In the petri dish beyond the lens, the miraculous 'cell division' Professor Harrison spoke of did not exist. What was there were only the blackened, rotting remnants of cells that had crumbled and lost their form. Far from dedifferentiation, it was just the dregs of decaying organic matter, crumbling in the chemicals and turning into black sludge. It wasn't the beginning of life, but merely fragments of bitterly 'dead' chunks of meat.

"How is it? Do you see it? Isn't that squirming vitality wondrous?" Professor Harrison shouted in Jason's ear, spitting as he spoke.

Jason slowly raised his head and looked at Professor Harrison. White spit had dried around the professor's mouth, and his skin was crumbling dryly like an old man who had died days ago. His pupils had long since lost their focus.

Jason finally realized. Even an elite intellectual like a biotechnology professor couldn't endure this horrific reality and had fallen into a perfect mass hallucination.

In order for a cell to divide and grow, the energy of a 'living' mother cell is essential. But the males trapped in this city right now were **'already dead phantoms'** moving only by inertia, unable to produce a single drop of blood themselves. Applying electricity to cells stripped from a corpse would never make them grow into a new life.

A mad scientist looking at rotting somatic cells and believing they are dividing. And the men outside, deceived by that fake life creation play, willingly volunteering to be slaves.

Jason suppressed the cold nausea rising from his gut and quietly stepped back from the microscope.

"Yes... It's wondrous, Professor. It's growing rapidly."

Jason replied with a hollow voice and turned toward the mechanical room door. The smell of death leaked between his lips as he spat the lie.

Exiting the mechanical room, Jason stared at the firmly shut iron door for a long time. Torture by hope is the cruelest punishment. The day the whole world learns that the cells in the rotting culture fluid are not life, even the last string of reason holding this camp together will snap. With the stopped senses of his entire body, Jason intuitively felt that the end of this disgusting and futile play of ghosts trying to conceive life was not far away.
