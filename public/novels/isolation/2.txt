## Episode 2: Unseen Beasts

"This scar... You... It was you. You’re the bitch who killed my daughter...!"

The man’s hoarse voice ripped through the freezing air of the underground ruin.

I felt his rough hands shoot through the darkness, aiming straight for my throat. The stale stench of sweat and the shallow breath of an animal losing control pierced my nose.

"Aaaah! Help me! He’s crazy!"

I let out an ear-piercing scream and tumbled to the floor.

But beneath the perfectly calculated shriek, my heartbeat was chillingly calm. This pitch-black void, stripped of all vision, was the perfect hunting ground for someone playing the helpless prey.

Crash!

His heavy body pinned me down, rolling over the rough stones. As his clumsy fingers clawed desperately at my neck, I thrashed around, feigning sheer panic. But in the chaos, I buried both my thumbs precisely into the radial nerves on the insides of his wrists.

"Help! Somebody, please... Ugh!"

Even as I spat out desperate cries for help, I shifted my weight into my fingertips and viciously crushed his nerves.

The sickening sensation of joints twisting. A breathy scream—"Gah!"—burst from his lungs. His hands, seconds away from strangling me, dropped limply like a puppet with cut strings.

"Hey! What the hell are you doing?! Calm down!"

Two other figures rushed over in the dark.

Someone’s strong arm hooked the man by the collar and dragged him off me. I didn't miss the chance. I crawled backward, playing the fragile woman hyperventilating from terror.

"Sob... Haa... He suddenly... He touched my scar... and said I did something to his daughter..."

"Y-You literal demon! Let go! That bitch took my daughter... Aaaargh!"

He thrashed wildly, but there was more fear than anger in his screams.

Just seconds ago, in the absolute dark, he had felt the monster hiding behind the mask of a weak middle-aged woman flawlessly neutralize both his arms. But to the others, who couldn't see a thing, he was just a lunatic on a rampage.

"Stop it, both of you! This isn't the time to kill each other!"

Another woman’s sharp voice echoed through the cavernous space.

Her shout brought back the heavy silence. Only the man’s ragged panting and my shallow, fake sobs clashed in the air.

Curled up tight on the rough stone floor, I carefully felt around with trembling hands.

As my fingertips swept across the cold stone, they brushed against something heavy and foreign.

It wasn't a rusty bar or a rock.

It was a smooth, metallic texture. A perfectly honed, razor-sharp blade. A hunting knife—intentionally tossed into the dark by whoever wanted to see us slit each other's throats.

Gripping the cold handle of the knife, I smiled soundlessly into the abyss.
