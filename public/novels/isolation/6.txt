## Episode 6: The Architect's Smile

The blinding white light violently exposed the naked reality of the centuries-old ruin.

As my eyes watered from the forced restoration of my sight, the first thing I saw through the blur was a state-of-the-art hospital bed sitting squarely in the middle of the rough stone floor.

And on it lay a grotesquely emaciated girl, completely motionless. She was still hooked up to the cardiac monitor that had just flatlined with that horrific, endless shriek.

"Eun-ji... Eun-ji!"

The man whose arms I had crushed crawled frantically across the floor, throwing himself at the bed. He pulled the cooling corpse of the young girl into his arms and began to howl like a wounded animal.

It was the exact name on the paperwork—the girl whose surgical insurance payout I had denied.

But as someone who had spent her entire life calculating human lives as mere numbers, it wasn't the sight of a dead girl that made my heart drop.

It was the "fourth person." The man sitting comfortably in a leather chair behind a thick, bulletproof glass control room on the other side of the bars, leisurely watching this bloodbath unfold.

A custom-tailored suit. Arrogantly crossed legs.

It was my ex-husband—the predecessor I had shoved off a cliff three years ago by cutting his brakes so I could take the CEO seat.

I thought he was rotting in hell, but here he was, perfectly alive, flashing a chilling smile at me through the glass.

This wasn't a random terror attack. From the very beginning, this was a massive slaughterhouse designed exclusively to hunt and break me.

"Starting to grasp the situation, Madam CEO?"

The doctor whispered dryly from right behind me.

I didn't even need to turn around. I could feel her practically pressing against my spine, undoubtedly holding her own razor-sharp hunting knife, ready to strike.

"Beg for your life. Use your precious numbers and probabilities to calculate a way out of this hell."

The cold tip of her knife poked my lower back through my thin cardigan.

In front of me was the perfect trap built by my dead husband; behind me was another predator ready to cut my throat. It was obvious they were working together.

Standing in the dead center of absolute despair, I slowly straightened my spine.

Readjusting my grip on the knife hidden in my sleeve, I flashed the brightest, most chilling smile of my entire life.

"The calculation is already done."
