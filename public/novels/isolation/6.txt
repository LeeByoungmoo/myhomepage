# PRESSURE
## Episode 6: Eruption

---

Time seemed to stop. 

The falling debris hung in mid-air as if nailed there, and the screaming kids running down the hallway moved with agonizing slowness, like ants trapped in jelly. 

The air was so heavy it was impossible to breathe. It was hard to tell if gravity had inverted or if space itself was warping. 

And at the center of this impossible phenomenon stood Ian. 

"Hhk..."

Blake lay flat on his stomach, gasping like a fish out of water. Barely a minute ago, he was a terrifying Level 7, systematically demolishing the Low-Tier building with his Pressure. But now, under the crushing weight bearing down on him, he was nothing more than an insect. 

*What the... what is...*

Blake tried to lift his head, but the excruciating pressure on his spine made him feel like he was going to snap in half. All he could do was roll his eyes. Into his line of sight stepped Ian, standing tall with blood trickling down his face. 

If a demon were to descend upon the earth, it would probably look like that. 

Space rippled around Ian's body. It wasn't just light refraction. The density of the air was so compressed that spacetime itself was warping. 

*Vwoooom—*

A low, resonant vibration began to shake the entire high school campus. 
And then, a miracle—or a disaster—occurred. 

The exterior walls and concrete pillars of the Low-Tier building that Blake had shattered began to slowly, very slowly... float up into the air. 

"Holy shit..."

Leo muttered blankly, slumped on the floor. The multi-ton slab of concrete that was supposed to crush his skull was now rising toward the sky, as if caught in a reverse-gravity field. It wasn't just the concrete. Shards of broken glass, bent rebar, school desks, and chairs all drifted upward, trapped inside a massive zero-gravity dome. 

Every cubic inch of air within a 500-yard radius was entirely under Ian's absolute control. 

Outside the building, in the Top-Tier specialized zone, students and teachers suddenly collapsed, clutching their chests. The air simply refused to enter their lungs. It was the pure terror of being strangled by an invisible hand. It didn't matter if they were Level 5 or Level 7. 

This was violence on a completely different dimension. 
It was the arrival of an absolute 'King.' 

***

At that exact moment, in an underground bunker just outside Washington D.C., the headquarters of the Federal Pressure Control Bureau. 

Red warning lights flashed across a massive screen spanning an entire wall. The agents in the control room stared at their monitors in sheer horror. 

"Director! We're detecting an anomalous Pressure reading in Sector 3!"

Director Vance rushed into the control room, a deep crease between his brows. 

"Anomalous? What's the reading?"
"Sir... the instruments seem to be malfunctioning. The reading has broken past the theoretical limit."
"Malfunctioning? Reboot the system."
"We've cycled to the backup systems three times already! The results are exactly the same."

With trembling hands, an agent pulled a graph onto the main screen. 
Typically, when a Level 8 user went completely out of control, they generated around 150,000 hectopascals (hPa) of Pressure energy. It was a number capable of leveling an entire city block. 

But the graph on the screen was rocketing past 150,000. It broke 300,000, then 500,000, climbing vertically. 

"This... this wave pattern..." 

Director Vance's lips quivered. 
He had dedicated his entire life to controlling and researching Pressure users. He knew exactly what this wave meant. It was something that had never been observed in human history, but theoretical physicists had warned about it for decades—the 'Singularity.'

"Level 10." 

Vance muttered in a hoarse whisper. 
A dead silence fell over the control room, as if someone had thrown a bucket of ice water over the staff. 

"That's impossible, sir. Level 10 is purely theoretical! If a human body were subjected to that kind of Pressure, they'd be ripped to shreds instantly!" 

"Which is exactly why this isn't a malfunction." 

A grotesque mixture of terror and absolute ecstasy flashed in Vance's eyes. 

"Scramble a tactical strike team to Sector 3 right now. Whatever it takes, secure the epicenter of that wave. Bring them to me without a single scratch on them!"

***

Back at the school. 

The hundreds of tons of building debris floating in the air slowly drifted back down to their original positions, settling softly as if someone had gently placed them there. 

*Thump.*

A faint cloud of dust plumed into the air, but there was no secondary collapse. 
And just like that, the overwhelming storm of Pressure receded like the tide. 

"*Cough, gasp...*" 

The kids who had been suffocating collectively choked, hacking as they desperately sucked in air. 

Ian stumbled and fell to his knees. The blood from his nose had stained his t-shirt red. Every muscle in his body screamed in agony, and his vision was spinning. 

*What... what did I just do?*

He looked down at his own two hands. Nothing had physically changed. But he had felt it with absolute certainty. For that brief, fleeting moment, he knew he could have crushed the entire world into dust. 

"Ian... you..."

Leo was staring at Ian with terrified eyes. He wasn't looking at his pathetic friend who ate crappy cafeteria food and complained about the world with him every day. In Leo's eyes, there was only 'fear.' 

Ian's heart turned to ice. 
That look. 
It was the exact same horrific look his parents gave his sister, Emma. 

"No... Leo, I'm..."

Ian reached out a hand, but Leo flinched and scrambled backward. 
That tiny, instinctive retreat gouged a hole right through Ian's chest. 

Then it happened. 

*Thwack-thwack-thwack!*

The deafening roar of rotor blades ripped through the air as three black, unmarked military helicopters descended over the football field. Tactical agents clad in all-black combat gear fast-roped from the choppers, surrounding the Low-Tier annex in seconds. 

The commander of the unit, who appeared to have the highest Pressure Level among them, walked toward Ian. He didn't draw a weapon. In fact, his demeanor was incredibly reverent and cautious. 

The commander took a knee right in front of Ian. 
With that single action, the chaotic, buzzing school fell dead silent. 

"Ian."

The commander spoke, his head bowed. 

"The Bureau has sent us to escort you."

It was the sound of the world turning upside down. 
A King was born. From the lowest of the lows, in the most terrifying form imaginable.
