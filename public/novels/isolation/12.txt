## Episode 12: 0.1 Seconds of Anatomy

The moment the cold tip of the blade pierced the red casing of the detonator, all the noise in my head was perfectly erased.

A complex maze of wires. But what caught my eye wasn't the colors of the cords, but a single, thick wire vibrating ever so slightly in sync with the boy's shallow breathing.

Slice.

The blood-slicked hunting knife tore through the wire without hesitation.

Sparks flew, and the acrid smell of gunpowder spread over the boy's thin chest. The frantic countdown on the bomb halted at 00:01 with a bizarre, mechanical dying sound.

"Oh God... Ma'am... thank you for saving me..."

The boy collapsed to the floor as if his legs had given out, weeping tears of pure relief.

But I didn't lower my knife. I just stood there, staring intently down at the boy's chest as he began to catch his breath.

"Not bad, darling. Didn't think that insane gamble would actually pay off."

My husband's voice through the speaker dripped with obvious displeasure.

His perfectly controlled chessboard had been smashed again by my cold, calculated intuition.

But my attention wasn't on his cheap provocation.

The bomb's lights had died, revealing the boy's bare skin underneath. Beneath the grotesquely stitched fake face, a thick, red incision scar running from his collarbone to his solar plexus seized my attention.

An unmistakable autopsy-style surgical scar. You didn't need medical training to recognize it.

My husband hadn't just planted a bomb on this fake son. It was clear that he had hidden the master key to the bulletproof control room deep inside the guts of this little rat.

"Ma'am...? W-what's wrong? Put the knife down..."

Primal fear flooded the boy's eyes again.

He instinctively realized that the eyes of the savior from seconds ago had transformed into the greed of a starving predator.

Readjusting the handle of my bloody knife, I whispered with chilling sweetness.

"What a shame. It turns out you still have some use left."

Before the boy could even scream, I roughly clamped my hand over his mouth and pressed the tip of the blade against the very top of his red incision scar.
