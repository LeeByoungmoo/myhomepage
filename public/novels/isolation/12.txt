# PRESSURE
## Episode 12: Liberators

---

A dark underground assembly hall. Hundreds of people stood in dead silence, looking up at the podium. 

Evelyn Thorne stood on the stage. She wasn't using a microphone, but her voice carried a strange reverberation of Pressure, striking clearly into every corner of the vast hall. 

"From the moment we are born, we are locked in a prison called 'Family.'"

Evelyn's eyes burned with a fierce light. 

"Think about it. Parents and children, brothers and sisters. What is the true nature of this supposedly sacred bond? It is Pressure. Those with high Pressure rule, and those with low Pressure submit. The weak are abused while walking on eggshells to please the strong, and the strong become monsters, crushing the ones they love because they can't control their own power. This is the true face of the hell we call 'Family.'"

Low gasps and muffled sobs echoed from the crowd. Most of them were victims of domestic violence caused by Pressure disparities, or conversely, those suffering from the guilt of having unintentionally hurt their own family members. 

"There is only one solution. As long as Pressure inequality exists, the institution of the blood-tied family is nothing but an evil custom. We must abolish the family! When a child is born, they must be strictly separated based on their Pressure Level, and communities must be formed only by those with similar strength. A world where the strong cannot trample the weak, and the weak do not have to fear the strong. That is the true freedom we Liberators dream of!"

Thunderous applause erupted from the crowd. 

In the very back row of the assembly hall. 
Ian stood with his coat collar popped, arms crossed, watching the spectacle unfold. 

He had come here at Evelyn's invitation. Her logic was perfectly cold and undeniably sharp. Until just a few days ago, Ian had harbored the exact same thoughts. A family with unequal Pressure is a living hell. 

After the assembly ended, Ian sat across from Evelyn in an empty green room behind the stage. 

"Well, Ian? Do you understand our cause now?" 
Evelyn asked as she poured tea. 

"I understand your anger." Ian said, taking the cup. "But your solution is insane. Force people of similar Levels to live together? That's not freedom. It's just a different kind of internment camp."

The smile vanished from Evelyn's lips. She slammed the teapot down roughly on the coffee table. 

*Crrrack—*
The surface of the table began to splinter. On her wrist, exposed beneath her sleeve, were horrific burn scars, as if she had been branded by fire. 

"Internment camp? You think you know what a real internment camp is?"

Evelyn's voice was laced with a sharp, unprecedented rage. 

"My mother was a Level 9. I was a Level 4. Every time my mother got angry, she would grab my arm and force her boiling Pressure into me. When I screamed, she called it 'discipline' that I just had to endure because we were family. Family? Blood? Just because of that one damn word, I couldn't run away. I had my flesh burned in that hell until I was twenty years old!"

Tears welled up in her eyes, but her Pressure was swirling around the room like a violent storm. 

"If I had just been able to strip away the shell of 'Family,' I would have handed that woman over to the police years ago and lived my own life! It was blood that kept me chained in hell!"

Ian sat still and took the brunt of her anger. Her scars were real. Her pain was agonizingly justified. 

But still. 

"So you want to just abolish the family entirely."
Ian said quietly. 

"Then what are you going to do about kids like Emma? Kids who get uncontrollable power at three years old and cry in terror because they don't even know who they're hurting. If you throw a bunch of monsters with the same Level into a room together, are those kids ever going to learn how to love?"

Evelyn bit her lip, seemingly at a loss for words. 

Ian stood up from his chair. 

"Because you were hurt, you've just chosen the easiest way not to get hurt again. You refuse to form a real relationship with anyone. That's not liberation. That's escapism."

"What... what the hell do you know?!" 
Evelyn slammed her hands on the table and screamed. Her massive Level 8 Pressure crashed toward Ian like a tidal wave. 

But Ian didn't even blink. With a casual wave of his hand, Evelyn's Pressure wave scattered and melted away like snow. It was a demonstration of absolute difference in power. 

"I hugged my monster of a little sister."
Ian said, grabbing the doorknob and looking back over his shoulder. 

"It turns out she didn't need a cold internment camp. She just needed an older brother who wasn't afraid of her. I won't be joining your revolution."

Ian walked out of the green room. 
The logic of The Liberators was flawed. But at the same time, the government's shameless complacency wasn't the answer either. 

On his walk home, Ian's smartphone buzzed. 
[Director Vance, Federal Pressure Control Bureau]

"Yeah, this is Ian."
- *Have you given our offer some thought, Ian? The federal government is anxiously awaiting your decision.*

"Before I make a decision, I want to see exactly what you mean by 'protection' and 'research' with my own eyes. Take me to the Bureau."

- *Ah, an excellent choice. We will send a car for you first thing tomorrow morning.*

The call disconnected. 
Having seen the true face of the extremists, Ian's next target was the very heart of the federal system that controlled this entire Pressure-driven society.
