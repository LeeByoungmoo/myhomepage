# PRESSURE
## Episode 20: Atmospheric Pressure at Sea Level (Finale)

---

One year had passed since that incident. 

The 'vacuum explosion' Ian created wasn't permanent. Over time, people's Pressure returned very slowly, little by little. 

But the events of that night had changed many things. 

During the brief few days when everyone's Pressure hit zero, the sheer incompetence of the government and the inhumane experiments conducted by the Bureau were exposed to the world. Director Vance was arrested for child abuse and abuse of federal power, and the Federal Pressure Control Bureau was completely dismantled. 

'The Liberators' also lost their momentum. Having experienced the sudden, terrifying loss of the overwhelming power they had been so proud of, they learned to look at problems with reason rather than force. It was said that Evelyn Thorne had stopped her extremist terrorism and opened a counseling center to help families suffering from Pressure-related domestic issues. 

And then, there was the Han family. 

"Hey, Ian! Are you wearing my t-shirt?!"

7:00 AM. Emma's irritated voice rang out across the living room of their cramped, rundown apartment. 

"Uh, no, this is mine! You bought the wrong one!"
Ian mumbled through a mouthful of toothpaste from the bathroom. 

"Don't give me that! The one with the tiger stripes is mine!"

Emma stomped over and kicked the bathroom door. 
*Bang!* 

The door rattled in its frame, but it didn't break. It wasn't because Emma's Pressure hadn't fully returned. She was mad, sure, but she no longer laced her physical actions with Pressure. She was just throwing a tantrum and bickering with her older brother like any ordinary middle school girl. 

"Keep it down, you two, it's too early for this!"
Sarah yelled from the kitchen, banging her spatula against a frying pan where she was cooking eggs. 

"And Ian, just let your sister have it! Emma, watch your tone when you talk to your brother!"

The mother who used to tremble and walk on eggshells around Emma was nowhere to be found. She had now become the absolute tyrant(?) of the kitchen, scolding her kids and nagging them constantly. 

In the corner of the living room, Sam was giggling as he rode on David's shoulders. David didn't wear a suppressor collar anymore. His Level 6 Pressure had returned, but he didn't use it to suppress his family. He only used it to carry heavy water jugs or to hover Sam in the air to play with him. 

The apartment living room was cramped, messy, and loud. 
But it was perfectly peaceful. 

Ian quickly rinsed his face, wiped it with a towel, and walked out into the living room. 
His Level 10 power had completely vanished after devouring everything that day. The doctors diagnosed that Ian's Pressure circuits had collapsed on themselves, leaving him permanently as a Null. 

But Ian wasn't sad about that diagnosis at all. 
In fact, he felt incredibly light. Having thrown off the heavy weight of the crown, he was now just a real, ordinary seventeen-year-old high school kid. 

Before heading out the front door for school, 
Ian stopped and stared at the old barometer hanging on the wall next to the shoe rack. 

The needle was pointing exactly at the number 1. 

1 atm. 
Standard atmospheric pressure at sea level. 

It was the most perfect, comfortable weight of air for a human being to breathe in and out. It was a normal, universal weight—one that crushed no one, and allowed no one to be crushed. 

"Ian! Hurry up! We're gonna be late!"
Emma yelled from the bottom of the stairs, her voice laced with annoyance. 

"Yeah, yeah, I'm coming!"

Ian flashed a grin at the barometer, pushed the door open, and stepped out with a light heart. 
The crisp morning air filled his lungs. 

Within the universe of their family, they had finally learned how to breathe. 

*(End)*
