## Episode 17: Bloodline from the Grave

The cold, red laser pointer rested perfectly on the center of the cross-shaped scar.

A fifth shadow stepped out from the dense darkness, aiming a loaded weapon. At the arrival of this uninvited guest, even the horrific screams and the stench of acid filling the ruin seemed to halt for a second.

"Twitch a single finger, and I'll blow your head off."

The shadow took another step into the blinding white light, finally revealing its face.

A horrific tire track scar slashed across his right eye, and a grotesque metallic screech echoed against the stone floor with every step he took on his prosthetic leg.

The moment I saw him, all the rational circuits in my head screamed and shattered.

Ten years ago, my biological son, whom I had shoved off a cliff in a car with cut brakes. My own flesh and blood, whom I thought was permanently crushed inside that despairing chunk of metal, had returned alive with the face of an apex predator.

"It's been a long time, Mother."

The barrel of my son's gun was aimed at the first rival's head, but his cold, one-eyed gaze pierced right through me.

My husband melting alive in the glass box. The first rival consumed by 15 years of revenge. None of them were the true masterminds of this hellish game.

Everyone thought they held the leash, but the real monster gripping the deadliest noose from the very bottom of this ruin was the bastard I birthed myself.

"You... you crazy son of a bitch!"

The moment the rival flinched and twitched the hand holding the master key, I didn't miss the millimeter of a gap.

Using the dead girl's corpse as a shield, I threw myself at the man like a wild beast.

The red master key slipped from his blunt fingers and bounced off the rough stone floor with a heavy clatter.

And simultaneously—

BANG!

A sharp gunshot ripped through the desolate silence of the ruin, and hot, sticky drops of blood splattered violently across my cheek.
