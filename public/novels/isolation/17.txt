# PRESSURE
## Episode 17: The Man Who Chose to Be Weak

---

As the silver suppressant collar shattered and rolled across the floor, the air surrounding David began to expand with an unnatural, violent force. 

It was Pressure that had been suppressed for nine long years. 
The Level 6 Pressure that tore through his veins wasn't explosive or devastatingly destructive like Ian's Level 10 or Emma's Level 8. David's Pressure was like a finely honed blade. It was the functionally condensed muscle of a veteran Enforcer, trained solely for the purpose of close-quarters combat and lethal suppression. 

"You... you son of a bitch!"

The squad leader dropped Emma's hair and swung his rifle toward David. 

*BANG! BANG! BANG!*

Three ultrasonic suppressor rounds flew toward David. 
But David didn't dodge. He concentrated his Pressure into a single point on his right forearm and swatted the incoming rounds away with his bare arm. 

*Clang! Clang-clang!*

The bullets ricocheted into the ceiling. The tactical agents' eyes widened in sheer disbelief. They had seen users project Pressure as a wide shield before, but using physical strikes to deflect the trajectory of specialized ammunition was a legendary combat technique that only existed in training manuals. 

"Sitting around rotting for nine years... my joints are a little stiff."

David cracked his neck side to side, popping the joints. 
In the next second, David's silhouette blurred, and he reappeared right in front of the squad leader's face. 

*THWACK!!*

David's fist slammed into the squad leader's jaw. The Pressure-infused punch crumpled the tactical helmet like a tin can, launching the massive man into the air. 

"Captain!"

The remaining six agents opened fire with their rifles simultaneously. 
David grabbed the unconscious squad leader by the tactical vest, using him as a human shield against the barrage of bullets as he rolled across the living room floor. 

"Ian!!"
David roared, his face covered in blood. 

"Take Emma, your mother, and Sam, and run! Right now!"

"Dad!"
Ian screamed. Suppressor rounds had grazed David's shoulder and thigh, tearing chunks of flesh away. Blood was splattering across the floor. 

"I'll stay. If I use my power..."
"No!!"

David shouted desperately as he snapped the knee of a second agent with a focused burst of Pressure. 

"If you use your power here, your family gets hurt! Your power isn't controlled yet. You'll kill these bastards, but your family is in this room!"

Those words gouged a hole in Ian's heart. 
Level 10. That colossal power wasn't a precision instrument he could use to take out a single enemy. If Ian detonated his Pressure here, the tactical squad would be vaporized, but the dilapidated motel would collapse, and Emma, Sarah, and Sam standing right behind him wouldn't survive it. 

He had the strongest power in the world, but ironically, it was too massive to protect the things that mattered most to him. 

"This is a father's job."
David said, panting heavily. His body was already reaching its limit. Unleashing his Pressure after nine years of suppression was tearing his aged blood vessels apart. 

"The father who left you in hell for nine years... let me carry the burden for my family, just this one last time."

Hot tears welled up in David's eyes. 
He looked at Ian, and for the first and last time, he gave his son the proud, confident smile of a true father. 

"Go! Now!!"

Ian gritted his teeth. 
He hoisted the injured Emma onto his back and grabbed his trembling mother's hand. Sarah was clutching Sam tightly to her chest. 

"Dad... you have to, you have to come back."

Instead of answering, David let out a feral roar and charged headlong into the remaining four tactical agents. Behind him, Ian and the rest of the family threw themselves through the shattered sliding glass door and disappeared into the darkness of the night. 

Behind them, horrific explosions and the sickening sounds of brutal close-quarters combat tore through the night sky. 

To buy his family the time they needed to escape, the man who had chosen to be weak for nine years willingly threw himself into the jaws of the monsters, trading his life for theirs.
