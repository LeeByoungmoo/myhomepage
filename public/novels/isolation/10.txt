## Episode 10: Voice from Hell

"Mom? Mom, is that you...?"

A young boy's voice echoing in the empty void. It was the voice of the son I had killed with my own hands ten years ago.

The red digital timer blinked down to '00:58, 00:57,' staining my retinas the color of blood.

A normal mother would have collapsed on the spot, wailing in guilt or driven mad by terror.

But what crawled up my spine wasn't remorse. It was an uncontrollable, grotesque sense of excitement.

The dead do not return. This was either a fake audio track pieced together by my husband to psychologically break me, or a cheap trap.

"That's your kid's voice! Your son is in there!"

The grieving father, sprawled next to his daughter's body, screamed and pointed at the dark doorway.

I didn't answer. Letting my bleeding left arm hang limp, I squeezed the knife in my right hand to its absolute limit and glared at the red timer.

The numbers blurred past '00:30,' pulsing frantically.

It was obvious that once the time ran out, lethal gas would flood this narrow passage, or the iron doors would seal shut forever. Without a shred of hesitation, I walked straight into the blood-scented darkness.

"Mom... I'm cold. It's too dark in here..."

The boy's trembling voice called out again from deep within the tunnel.

This wasn't being piped in from the control room speakers. It meant 'something' that sounded exactly like my son was actually alive and breathing at the end of this suffocating corridor.

*00:10, 00:09...*

Behind me, the massive iron doors began to slide shut with a horrific metallic screech. Forgetting the agony in my torn arm, I threw myself blindly toward the source of the sound in the dark.

*Slam!*

The exact moment the doors sealed behind me and pitch-black darkness swallowed me whole, I snatched the back of the neck of a small silhouette huddled under the red light of the timer.

The warm body heat of a young boy. The chillingly familiar feel of those thin shoulders.

"Gotcha, you little rat."

I laughed like a beast and raised my knife high. But the moment the silhouette slowly lifted its head and locked eyes with me, the cold smile on my lips shattered completely.
