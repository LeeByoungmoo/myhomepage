## Episode 19: Baptism of Hellfire

The massive pillar of fire surged up the vertical shaft like a snake, threatening to scorch my retinas.

In an instant, the oxygen inside the ruin ignited, and every breath I took brought the agonizing sensation of my lungs burning dry.

"Well, jump. You should save the life you love so much."

Through the cold metal pressed against my forehead, the grotesque laughter of the son I abandoned ten years ago mixed with the roaring flames.

A normal mother would have begged for mercy at gunpoint or collapsed from the terror of the fire.

But with my blood-dripping left hand, I violently grabbed the scorching hot barrel of the gun. And swinging my right hand, I ruthlessly slashed his wrist with my hunting knife just as his finger tightened on the trigger.

"Gaaaargh!"

My son's scream of agony mixed with a stray gunshot that echoed through the ruin as the bullet missed its mark.

I didn't waste that split second. Charging like a beast, I grabbed him by the collar.

"When you go to hell, you always take a companion."

Smiling like a demon, I hugged his thrashing body and threw us both straight down into the black, roaring flames of the vertical shaft.

The deafening roar of the fire. The acrid smell of my hair and cardigan crisping instantly.

In the split second of our endless fall, I instinctively used my son's body as a meat shield, pinning him beneath me.

CRASH!

With a horrific shattering sound, we smashed onto the cold iron grate at the bottom of the shaft.

My son's metal leg exploded into pieces, sending shrapnel tearing into my thigh, but thanks to my 'meat shield,' I was miraculously still breathing.

"Hah... hah..."

Swallowing the pain of my shattered body, I crawled across the rough grate.

In the darkness where the red flames had died down, I reached out my bloody hand toward the faint, blinking red light of the 'master key.'

But right before my fingertips brushed the plastic.

A rough dress shoe stepped out of the darkness and mercilessly stomped down on the red master key. The sound of our only hope of survival being crushed into powder echoed across the grate.

"Oh dear. The key broke. Whatever will we do, Madam CEO?"

Over the shattered remains of the master key, a chilling voice announcing the 'true ruler' of this massive slaughterhouse rained down from the dark.
