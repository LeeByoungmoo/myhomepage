# PRESSURE
## Episode 19: The Weight of Choice

---

The air across the battlefield sank with a crushing, unbearable weight. 
Level 10 Pressure wasn't just a physical shockwave. It was more like an 'abyss.' Even if they tried to gasp for breath, the air itself was bound to Ian's will, refusing to budge. 

"Ian!"

Evelyn Thorne cried out, clutching her chest in agony, a crazed smile spreading across her face. 

"I'm glad you're here! Look at this! Look at the pathetic state of this government! Crush this rotting world that breeds children like livestock using Pressure! If you stand with us, we can shatter the chains of 'Family' and build an entirely new world!"

On the opposite side, Director Vance struggled to his knees and screamed in desperation. 

"Don't listen to her! They are domestic terrorists! Ian, execute them. The federal government will deify your family! I will give you everything you could possibly want. Your family can become the absolute rulers of this world, never to be suppressed by anyone!"

An extremist who wanted to destroy the family for liberation. 
A power broker who wanted to use Pressure to stand at the apex of a perfect caste system. 

Two temptations tangled around Ian as he stood in the burning plaza. Ian clenched his fists. If he just raised his hand and brought it down, both Evelyn Thorne and Director Vance would be pulverized into the concrete. That was the violent reality of Level 10. 

Ian's eyes drifted down to his father, David, lying on the ground. 

Covered in blood, David was slowly shaking his head. 
He wasn't speaking, but Ian could read exactly what his father wanted to say. 

*Don't become a monster.*

Ian looked down at his own two hands. 
What if he crushed them with his power right here? What if he killed The Liberators and destroyed the Bureau? 

In the end, he would just become another tyrant forcing his will onto others through sheer power. It would be no different than when his little sister, Emma, had forced their father to his knees and ruled over their dining table. The scale was just bigger; the cursed cycle of Pressure would continue forever. 

If power was the cause. 
If this 'unequal Pressure' was the root that turned families into living hells, and turned people into terrorists and wardens. 

"You're both wrong."

Ian's low, heavy voice echoed across the plaza. 

"Family isn't hell because of blood. It's hell because someone is strong and someone is weak. And the government is hell because it tries to control that power."

Ian slowly spread his arms out to his sides. 
The spatial distortion rippling around him suddenly stopped. Instead, an energy like a pitch-black singularity began to vortex right around Ian's heart. 

"Ian... no! That's...!"
Sensing what was happening, David screamed in horror. 

Ian didn't project his colossal Level 10 Pressure outward. Instead, he began to collapse all of that immense Pressure **inward**, into himself. 

*Vwooooosh—!*

The atmosphere let out a bizarre, shrieking wail. 
A massive vacuum formed with Ian at the epicenter. The Pressure that had been suffocating everyone in the vicinity was instantly sucked inward. 

But it wasn't just the air being pulled in. 

"My... my Pressure...!"

Evelyn Thorne looked down at her hands in sheer terror. The ferocious Level 8 Pressure waves that had made her like a rabid beast were deflating like a popped balloon. 

Director Vance also rolled on the ground, screaming. The Level 6 Pressure that sustained his body was evaporating without a trace. 

Every single user within a ten-mile radius was drained of their power. The massive vacuum black hole Ian had created was devouring all the unequal power in the world. 

"If power is the problem."

Blood vessels burst across Ian's body, sending up a fine red mist. The physical agony of his body inversely swallowing Level 10 energy was beyond human imagination. 

"Then we all... just need to become weak."

*FWOOOOOSH—!!!*

With a blinding flash of light, a silent explosion went off. No buildings collapsed, no flames erupted. But everyone in the plaza collapsed to the ground, as if gravity itself had momentarily vanished. 

The strongest boy in the world 
had made the most foolish choice possible: erasing all the power in the world. 

Only empty air remained in the plaza. Pure, ordinary air, completely devoid of anyone's Pressure or suppression.
