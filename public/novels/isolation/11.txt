## Episode 11: The Stitched Ghost

The face revealed under the red glow of the timer.

It was definitely the face of the son I had killed ten years ago. But the cold smile on my lips instantly plummeted to rock bottom.

Following the boy's cheeks, jawline, and eyes, grotesque, red surgical scars were stitched together like a dense spiderweb.

"Ma'am... I did exactly what I was told, so you're not going to hurt me now, right...?"

The boy looked up at me with eyes full of sheer terror, his lips trembling.

The voice that had just called me "Mom" was actually coming from a tiny, sophisticated speaker implanted near his collarbone, projecting a chillingly distorted audio file.

To completely break me, the husband sitting in the control room had found an orphan of similar build, mutilated his face, and stitched it together grotesquely to look exactly like my dead son.

Beep— Beep— Beep!

In that moment, an ear-piercing warning siren erupted from the timer hitting '00:00'.

Simultaneously, beneath the thick padded vest wrapped tightly around the boy's torso, a terrifying red light began to flash frantically.

It was a time bomb.

From the very start, my husband intended to trap this fake son and me in the dark and blow us into a mist of blood. Where the timer numbers disappeared, a new, final 10-second countdown flashed into existence.

"Please help me, Ma'am! I don't want to die!"

The boy grabbed my bloody left arm, sobbing and clinging to me.

Chewing through the numbness of the pain in my mauled arm, I coldly stared down at the complex tangle of detonator wires wired into the boy's vest.

"Don't cry. If you die here, that lunatic out there wins."

I adjusted my grip on the blood-slicked hunting knife.

Without a single moment of hesitation, I drove the cold tip of the blade straight toward the furiously blinking detonator.
