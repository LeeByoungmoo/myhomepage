## Episode 16: The First Ghost

The cross-shaped burn scar mangled into the back of his neck.

It was definitively the mark of my first rival, the man I paid a hitman to burn alive 15 years ago to secure my position as CEO. The acrid stench of my husband's flesh melting alive inside the glass box mixed with the cold air of the ruin, stabbing my lungs.

"I guess the hitman didn't burn the corpse thoroughly enough."

My brain's processing stopped for exactly one second. Instead of collapsing in uncontrollable terror, I readjusted my grip on the bloody knife and let out a chilling, metallic smile.

"Still arrogant and acting like an animal. That's what made hunting you so enjoyable."

The man callously stepped over Hannah's corpse and walked closer to me. "This chunk of meat was just excellent bait I bought from a corrupt morgue. To perfectly control your idiot husband, blinded by revenge."

The shocking truth that even playing the pitiful, grieving father was nothing more than a meticulously calculated act.

Me, my husband, and now this man. From the very beginning, there wasn't a single person with an ounce of intact humanity inside this massive slaughterhouse.

"Well then, now that you know who holds the real leash, I'll give you the chance to snap your own neck."

With his blunt thumb, the man pressed the red button on the master key one long time.

The massive stone floor in the dead center of the ruin where we stood began to split apart with a heavy, grinding roar.

*Crack, rrrrrumble.*

The floor opened to reveal a pitch-black, bottomless vertical shaft.

And from that dark abyss, a horrific, choking 'smell of gasoline' surged upward—a stench far worse than rotting animal blood. The exact same smell from the day I burned this man alive 15 years ago.

But in that exact moment, a cold, red laser pointer landed perfectly dead center on the man's cross-shaped scar.

"Don't move. Take your hand off that master key."

From the deepest darkness in the corner of the ruin, the 'fifth survivor' stepped out, aiming a loaded gun directly at the man's head.
