# PRESSURE
## Episode 16: Her Side

---

The warm air that had filled the living room was instantly replaced by the acrid smell of gunpowder and cold, murderous intent. 

There were exactly seven tactical agents who stepped over the shattered remains of the front door. Every single one of them was a veteran Enforcer, Level 5 or higher. The silver tactical rifles in their hands were loaded not with bullets, but with specialized ultrasonic rounds designed to disrupt and scatter Pressure. 

"Ian Han. You are under arrest for treason and the theft of a classified national asset."
The squad leader said, aiming his muzzle directly at the space between Ian's eyes. 

"Lethal force is authorized if you resist. We cannot guarantee the safety of your family."

Ian stepped forward, shielding his family behind his back. 
Just as his Level 10 Pressure began to seep out from his body. 

"Move."

A small but sharp voice rang out from behind Ian. 
It was Emma. 

She pushed Ian aside and stepped right in front of the squad leader's rifle. 

"This is my brother's fight. You don't belong here."
Ian urgently tried to grab Emma's wrist, but she shook her head. 

"Nine years." 
Emma glared at the tactical agents, her voice a low growl. 
"I spent nine years turning this house into a living hell. Smashing things, flipping tables, screaming at everyone."

A vicious, dark blue vortex of Pressure began to swirl around Emma's feet. 
It wasn't the uncontrollable rage of the past, the tantrums she threw just to get attention or protect herself from being hurt. 

For the first time in her life, she was summoning the pure, lethal intent of a Level 8 not for herself, but to protect her 'Family.' 

"My brother *finally* managed to fix the house I spent nine years breaking..."

*Vwooooosh—!*

Emma's eyes burned bright blue with raw Pressure energy. 

"And you scumbags dare to stomp in here with your muddy boots and try to break it again?!"

The moment Emma stomped her foot, the air in the living room expanded explosively. Two tactical agents screamed as they were thrown backward, and the concrete walls cracked and splintered, sending debris flying everywhere. 

"Open fire! Fire the suppressors!"
The squad leader yelled in a panic. 

*Thwip-thwip-thwip!*

Ultrasonic rounds ripped through the living room. 
As the bullets struck the barrier of Pressure, Emma's force began to waver and dissipate. 

The government's specialized weapons had been developed with billions of dollars specifically to neutralize high-level users. Even as a Level 8, it was impossible for a young girl to withstand a barrage of suppressor rounds fired by seven highly trained adults with her bare body. 

"Ugh...!"

Emma dropped to her knees. Crimson blood poured from her nose and mouth. 
As her Pressure barrier was neutralized, one of the agents delivered a brutal, heavy kick with his combat boot straight into Emma's stomach. 

*Thud!*

"Emma!!"
Sarah screamed. 

Emma collapsed on the floor, hacking and coughing violently. It felt like her ribs were broken. 

"Ian, don't do it..."
Emma yelled, forcefully biting her bloody lip as she looked at her brother. 

"If you use your power, they're really going to label you a terrorist... Just let me be the bad guy. If I'm the only one who goes to prison... our family can live a normal life..."

Twelve years old. 
Realizing that she had become a burden, the little girl was throwing herself to the wolves as a sacrificial lamb. 

"Stupid brat. You think this ends just because you stepped up?"
The squad leader roughly grabbed a fistful of Emma's hair and yanked her head back. 

"Your whole family ends today. We don't leave the families of traitors alive."

The squad leader drew his sidearm and pressed the barrel against Emma's temple. 
Right at that moment. 

*Riiiiip—*

An unsettling sound of tearing metal echoed through the living room. 

"Let go of my daughter. Now."

It was David. 
With his bare hands, he had just ripped off the black metal collar—a permanent Pressure-suppressant device—from his own neck and thrown it on the floor. 

The man who, nine years ago, had chosen to suppress his own power and live as an ordinary citizen and an incompetent father, just to protect Emma. 

For the first time in nine years, he was unleashing his true power.
