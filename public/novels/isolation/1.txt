## Episode 1: Predator in the Dark

I woke up to a biting chill.

Total, suffocating darkness. The freezing dampness of centuries-old stone seeped through my thin cardigan.

My hand, tracing the rough frost on the cracked floor, brushed against something warm and soft.

Flinching muscle. A human cheek.

I could feel the frantic, shivering vibration of a jawbone trapped in my grip.

A shallow, rapid heartbeat pulsed against my fingertips from their neck. That pathetic, animalistic pulse tickled my hand in the best way possible. A sweet urge washed over me—how easy it would be to press my thumbs into their windpipe and end it right now.

Instead, I yanked my hand back as if I’d been burned.

Curling my body tightly against the rough stone floor, I let out a trembling, carefully calculated breath into the dark.

"Help... Please, is anyone there...?"

Silence. Then, the sound of heavy breathing and someone scrambling backward.

"W-Who are you?! Stay back!"

It was a man’s voice, raspy and cracked. A weak confession of absolute terror. Hiding a cold smile, I made my voice tremble even more.

"I... I just woke up too. I can't see anything. Please help me..."

As if our voices were the trigger, groans erupted from all corners of the pitch-black void.

"Ugh..."

"Where the hell are we...?"

Four of us in total. Someone had carefully calculated this and herded us like animals into a sealed box. I focused all my senses on the fingertips pressing into the floor.

It wasn't smooth concrete. It was massive, roughly hewn stone blocks, paired with the cold, rusty texture of thick iron bars.

An ancient underground ruin. Choosing a forgotten place like this over a modern facility reeked of deep, deliberate malice.

Rumble—

Suddenly, a massive vibration shook the ceiling, threatening to crush us.

The rhythmic screech of giant steel wheels. A subway. We had been buried alive in the deepest, dampest bottom of a glamorous city center, right beneath the feet of millions.

As the tremor faded, the suffocating silence returned.

Wearing my mask of a frail, terrified middle-aged woman, I pretended to shiver and carefully grabbed the edge of the man’s shirt.

"Sir... I'm scared. Please, let's stick together..."

"Wait."

The man snatched my wrist with bone-crushing force.

In the dark, his ragged breath drew so close it brushed my ear. His blunt fingers frantically traced the inside of my right wrist.

"This scar..."

His voice was shaking like a leaf. The terror from moments ago was instantly mutating into an uncontrollable, murderous rage.

"You... It was you. You’re the bitch who killed my daughter!"
