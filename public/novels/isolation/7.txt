## Episode 7: Blood in the Margin of Error

"The calculation is already done."

The moment the words left my mouth, I didn't lunge forward to escape. Instead, I threw my entire body backward, willingly impaling myself on the doctor’s cold blade.

Thwack. The freezing chunk of metal pierced my thin cardigan and buried itself deep into my side.

Pain flared hot enough to white out my vision, but it was a perfectly calculated wound. Well within the margin of error for survival.

"Are you crazy—?!"

My suicidal maneuver made the doctor's breath hitch. In that one second of panic, her grip on the handle loosened, and I spun around like a rabid animal.

With the hunting knife still in my hand, I drove the blade mercilessly into her thigh, just barely missing the femoral artery under her thin shirt.

"Aaaargh!"

Her flawless, gentle mask shattered instantly. A raw, horrific scream echoed through the cavernous underground ruin. Hot, sticky blood gushed over the back of my hand, creating a grotesque contrast under the blinding white surgical lights.

The doctor collapsed, clutching the knife buried in her leg, rolling in agony on the rough stone floor.

Clamping a hand tightly over my bleeding side, I staggered toward the bulletproof glass of the control room.

Behind the glass, my husband’s arrogant smile had frozen stiff.

His elegant, perfectly controlled revenge plot had just devolved into an uncontrollable, bloody mudfight in mere seconds, and he was visibly disgusted.

I smeared my bloody palm against the cold bulletproof glass.

Locking eyes with him, I smiled brightly and soundlessly mouthed the words: I'm going to kill you again.

Screeeech!

Suddenly, an ear-splitting microphone feedback tore through the air, and the speakers mounted around the ruin crackled to life. For the first time in three years, my husband’s arrogant, overly familiar voice rained down on the room.

"Still acting like an animal, darling. But your perfect calculations are missing one fatal variable."

As his dry voice faded, a massive iron door hidden in the deepest, darkest corner of the ruin began to grind open with a heavy metallic groan.

And from the crack of that open door, the bizarre growls of beasts—and dozens of flashing, feral eyes—poured out toward us.
