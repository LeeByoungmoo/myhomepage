# PRESSURE
## Episode 7: The Morning After

---

The world turned upside down. 

'First Ever Level 10 Recorded!'
'Pressure Monitoring Systems Paralyzed: The Boy Who Broke Human Limits.'
'Miracle or Disaster for the Low-Tiers? The Awakening of a 16-Year-Old Null.'

Every news channel, social media feed, and digital billboard in the city was plastered with Ian's name. The federal government designated Ian as a 'Class 1 National Asset' and completely locked down a three-mile radius around his high school. 

And that breaking news was currently blaring from the old CRT television inside the family's FEMA motel room. 

"What... what is this?"

Sarah muttered, her voice trembling as she gripped the remote. The screen was playing a looped clip of Ian stepping out of a black military chopper, being escorted into the Federal Bureau's headquarters. At the bottom of the screen, a bright red chyron flashed: [MEASUREMENT OFF CHARTS: ESTIMATED LEVEL 10]. 

David sat slumped on the edge of the motel bed, his face as pale as a sheet. 

He was a man who had given up his own power because he couldn't handle his Level 8 daughter. And now, his eldest son—the kid who had lived his entire life as a Null, the invisible shadow of this family—was a Level 10? 

*My boy... my son... how the hell was he surviving with that kind of power inside him?*

David's mind felt like it was going to explode from the confusion. Sixteen years. For sixteen years, enough energy to reduce the city to ashes had been boiling inside the body of that quiet, scrawny kid. 

Just then, the bathroom door opened. 
Emma stepped out into the main room. 

Her eyes immediately locked onto the TV screen. An anchor was pointing to a graph showing Ian's explosive Pressure spike. 

Sarah instinctively checked Emma's reaction. Her daughter was a child who always needed constant reassurance that she was the ultimate authority in the room. The sudden appearance of a brother whose power casually dwarfed her own might trigger a tantrum that could level the entire motel. 

"Em-Emma, sweetie. It's just fake news. You know how the media always exaggerates..."

Sarah desperately tried to make excuses, but Emma's expression was different today. 
It wasn't rage. It wasn't jealousy. 

Emma's pupils were shaking violently. 
It was a bizarre cocktail of emotions. 
The absolute **terror** of encountering a being stronger than herself. 
And... the strange, twisted **relief** that she was no longer the only 'monster' in the family. 

Emma stood silently in front of the TV, staring blankly at her older brother's pale face on the screen. 

***

That night. 

After hours of intense testing and interrogation at the Bureau, Ian finally returned to the motel. Two heavily armed tactical agents flanked him, acting as a close-quarters security detail. 

"This is far enough." 

Ian stopped in front of the motel room door and addressed the agents. His voice was low and calm, but the agents instinctively flinched and stepped back. 

"But sir, Director Vance insisted for the safety of your family..."
"I'll take care of my own family's safety. Stand down."

The moment Ian's gaze sharpened even a fraction of a degree, the airflow in the outdoor corridor stalled. The agents broke out in a cold sweat, silently bowed their heads, and retreated down the motel stairs. 

Ian stood in front of the door. 
Even just yesterday, opening this door was a terrifying chore. He always had to weigh how heavy the air was on the other side. 

But right now, Ian felt absolutely nothing. 
The Pressure leaking from Emma inside the room felt... impossibly weak and insignificant. It was like an elephant trying to feel the breath of an ant. 

*Click.* 

He opened the door. 

His entire family was gathered in the cramped room. David, Sarah, Emma, and Sam. 

The moment Ian took a step inside. 
The air froze solid. 

Every single pair of eyes snapped to Ian. It wasn't the indifference or the pitying looks he had received for sixteen years. 

It was pure, unadulterated **awe and terror**. 

Sarah swallowed hard and backed away toward the mini-fridge. David shot up from the edge of the bed but didn't dare take a step toward him. They weren't looking at him like a 'son.' They were looking at him like a natural 'disaster.' 

Ian's heart sank like a stone. 

*So this is what it feels like.*

This was what it meant to hold absolute power. He didn't have to do anything. Just by standing there and breathing, he turned everyone else in the room into a sinner begging for mercy. 

Ian shifted his gaze to Emma. 
Emma was gripping the armrest of the cheap armchair. Her knuckles were bone white. 

Ian slowly took a step toward her. 

*Flinch.*

Emma took one step backward. 
It was a tiny movement. Just a single step. 
But that one step was a formal declaration of surrender, completely overturning the nine-year history of the Han household. 

The Level 8 monster, the absolute tyrant of the family, instinctively felt terror and retreated in the presence of the new Level 10 King. 

Ian stopped in his tracks. 
He saw his own reflection in Emma's trembling eyes. 

To defeat the monster, he had become an even more terrifying monster himself. 

"I'm home."

Ian said, his lips dry and cracked. 
No one could answer him. Only four-year-old Sam blinked his eyes, completely unaware of what was happening. 

On the night the family's power dynamic was perfectly inverted, 
Ian held the greatest power in the world, but he had never been more thoroughly alone.
