## Episode 8: The Hungry Beasts' Meal

The sickening stench of animal sweat and rotting blood instantly swallowed the air.

As the heavy iron doors parted, a pack of massive, starved fighting dogs, their ribs jutting through their skin, surged into the blinding white light.

"You're insane... Aaaargh!"

The doctor, still on the floor with my knife in her leg, screamed and thrashed backward. But to a pack of starving beasts, the most stimulating bait was the fresh pool of blood currently spilling from her torn artery.

I pressed my hand harder against my bleeding side and backed away with chilling calm.

The largest dog leaped through the air and clamped its jaws onto the doctor's neck. With a sickening crunch, her screams were drowned out by a horrific gargling sound in less than a second.

Behind the bulletproof glass, my husband watched the bloodbath with a rapturous expression, like he was enjoying a first-class opera.

Even the fact that I had stabbed his own pawn and thrown her to the dogs was just a source of entertainment within his 'margin of error.'

But my eyes weren't on the woman being torn apart. They were locked on the dark void behind the iron doors where the beasts had just emerged from.

It was the only passage connecting this sealed ruin to my husband's control room. The answer to flipping this hunting ground upside down had to be hiding in that empty darkness.

I kicked off the rough stone floor and sprinted madly toward the pack of dogs.

The grieving father was desperately swinging a heavy metal bedframe to protect his daughter's corpse, scattering the beasts' attention. I used his distraction as my meat shield and charged.

"You better hurry, darling!"

My husband's mocking laughter ripped through the speakers. At the same time, the most grotesque, massive dog lingering at the back of the pack slowly turned its head toward me.

What the beast had in its mouth wasn't a chunk of the doctor's flesh.

It was my hunting knife, yanked straight from the woman's leg, now dripping with a sticky mixture of her blood and mine.

Stripped of my only weapon, locking eyes with a blood-soaked monster.

For the first time in my life, my perfectly controlled heartbeat froze ice-cold.
