# PRESSURE
## Episode 8: The Ones Who Come Knocking

---

The next morning, the first person to knock on Ian's door was Director Vance of the Federal Pressure Control Bureau. 

He was wearing an impeccably tailored Italian suit. He had dismissed his armed security detail outside and sat alone in the cramped living room of the family's FEMA motel. Even the steam rising from his paper cup of cheap coffee seemed to float in perfectly straight, disciplined lines. 

"It is an honor to meet you again, Ian. And your parents, of course."

Vance gave a polite, measured nod. 

"I'll get straight to the point. Ian's power exists on a scale that our current Pressure monitoring systems simply cannot comprehend. Level 10. This is a matter of paramount national security."

"So, what are you going to do with my son?" 
David asked, his voice hoarse. 

Vance offered a smooth, practiced smile. 
"Please, there's no need to be defensive. We have no intention of 'controlling' Ian. We want to 'protect' and 'support' him. We can provide top-tier housing, a lifetime pension, and if he wishes, privileges that rival a cabinet member's. All we ask in return is that he regularly reports to the Bureau to cooperate with our 'training' and 'research.'"

The subtext of his words was crystal clear. 
*We'll give you a gilded cage, as long as you become the ultimate weapon for the federal government.* 

Ian sat back against the cheap motel sofa, staring quietly at Director Vance. 
The old Ian wouldn't have even been able to meet the Director's eyes. But right now, Ian could clearly see the ripples of Pressure radiating from Vance's body. Level 6. It was a strong force, but if Ian wanted to, he could flatten the Director against the floor with a flick of his finger. 

"I'll think about it." 
Ian replied curtly. 

Vance's smile wavered, just a fraction of an inch. 
"Ian, this isn't exactly a choice of..."
"I said, I'll think about it."

The air in the room suddenly grew heavy. Ian had merely put a slight emphasis on one word, but Director Vance's breath hitched in his throat for a split second. Vance swallowed dryly, reached into his suit jacket, and respectfully placed a business card on the wobbly coffee table. 

"I look forward to your wise decision."

After Vance left, Ian went into the cramped motel bathroom to get some space. 
The screen of his smartphone flashed with an eerie light. It was a text message from a restricted number. 

[Do not become a dog for the government. They will lock you in a cage. We can give you true freedom. - The Liberators]

Ian deleted the message. 
'The Liberators.' A radical, anti-government, anti-family extremist group that had been quietly gaining traction among high-level users. Whether it was Director Vance or The Liberators, in the end, they all just wanted to use Ian's power for their own agendas. 

*Nobody actually cares about my family.*

He walked out of the motel and headed toward a nearby suburban park to clear his head. 
He was sitting on a bench, absentmindedly turning an unopened can of soda in his hands, when he heard familiar footsteps. 

It was Sienna. 
She still had that neat bob cut and that unreadable, emotionless face. 

"You turn the world upside down, and this is what you do for fun?"
She casually sat down next to him on the bench. 

"You got anything else to say to me? Like how I smell like burning, or how I'm a walking bomb?"
Ian snapped back, his tone defensive. 

"Yeah. I do have something to say."
Sienna opened her palm. Hovering just above it, the unopened soda can in her hand began to crumple without any physical contact, flattening into a disk of crushed aluminum. 

Ian's eyes widened. 
"You..."

"Level 7. That's my real classification."
She said it as if commenting on the weather. 

"Then why were you in the Low-Tier annex? You could've transferred to the Top-Tier academy and lived like royalty."

For the first time, a shadow of melancholy settled in Sienna's eyes. 

"My dad was a Level 7, too. My house was a warzone every single day. My dad beat my mom, my older brother got beat by my dad, and then he'd take it out by smashing everything in the house. The day I awakened as a Level 7, I could have become the king of that hell."

She tossed the crushed can into a nearby trash bin. 

"But I didn't. Because if I became the king, my family would just turn into terrified bugs crawling at my feet. So, I practiced masking my Pressure like my life depended on it. I chose to become a 'Nobody.' Because that was the smartest conclusion I could come up with."

Sienna looked Ian dead in the eye. 

"But you can't hide a scale like that. You've already put on the crown, Ian. Wake the hell up. The second you grip that power, your family is going to look at you like a monster."

Sienna's words rippled deep within Ian's chest. 

On his walk back to the motel. 
Ian watched as the leaves on the trees along the sidewalk shivered and stilled in perfect sync with his shifting mood. 

Without even realizing it, he was already finding a strange, intoxicating comfort in the absolute control this power gave him. 
He hadn't fully realized yet that this was the prologue to the most horrifying tragedy of all.
