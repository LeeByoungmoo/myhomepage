## Episode 15: The Real Owner of the Leash

"It took exactly three years for that stupid bastard to dig his own grave."

Kicking Eun-ji's corpse away like an insect, there was no longer a trace of desperate fatherly love on the man's face.

The mask of the weak, grieving victim he had worn just moments ago was perfectly ripped away.

The real master key, gripped in his blunt fingers, let out a cold metallic clink.

Inside the bulletproof glass box, bathed in blood-red emergency light, my husband was frantically pounding on the window, begging for his life. But his screams, muffled by the thick glass, made him look like a grotesque, gasping fish.

Beep.

The man casually pressed a button on his master key.

Instantly, a pitch-black liquid poured from the ventilation shafts in the ceiling of the glass box like a waterfall.

"Aaaaaaaagh!"

The screams leaking faintly through the glass were on an entirely different level now.

It wasn't just water. It was a lethal, corrosive acid that violently burned through clothes and skin the second it made contact. My husband was fighting a horrific battle, melting alive inside the perfect control room he had built.

"A real hunting dog always bites the wrist of the owner holding its leash."

Turning his back on the gruesome sight of his employer melting alive, the man walked deliberately toward me.

He was the 'true architect' who had fueled my husband's desire for revenge, forced him to build this massive underground ruin, and hijacked control at the critical moment to bury us all alive.

Swallowing the pain in my shredded left arm, I readjusted my grip on the blood-soaked knife.

I coldly analyzed the perfectly controlled muscles and stride of the man approaching me.

But under the glaring white lights, something familiar caught my eye near the nape of the man's neck.

A burn scar. Not the kind of mark you'd expect to see on an ordinary father who lost his daughter to an insurance scam.

"Don't tell me... You're..."

It was a highly distinct, cross-shaped burn scar—the exact same scar that belonged to my very first rival from 15 years ago, the man I had hired a hitman to burn alive so I could take the CEO seat.

The man stepped close enough to be in range of my knife and smiled coldly.

And the next words that spilled from the mouth of this ghost I thought was dead completely fried the rational circuits in my brain like a lightning strike.
