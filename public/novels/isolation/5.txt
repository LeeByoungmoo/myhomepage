## Episode 5: The Ghost Under the White Light

Beep— Beep— Beep—

The cardiac monitor stabbed at my eardrums. It was the exact soundtrack of the wards where I had signed away countless lives by denying their insurance claims.

The moment the sound hit the air, the doctor’s body—still pressed against my blade—shivered with pure amusement.

The machinery groaned again, gears interlocking as they violently twisted the heavy stone floor. The smell of crushed rock and dust violently flooded my sinuses.

"That sound... Eun-ji... That's the sound from my daughter Eun-ji's hospital room!"

The man whose arms I had crushed started screaming like a madman, dragging himself across the floor.

The sickening sound of his fingernails scraping and breaking against the stone blended horribly with the cold, sterile beeping.

He was the grieving father of one of the lives I had erased with a single sheet of paper.

Whoever built this hell had meticulously gathered the grudges from my past and shoved them into this tiny, dark box.

"Help! Someone, please turn that sound off!"

I threw myself flat on the floor, squeezing out another terrified, metallic scream.

But my right hand was still gripping the hunting knife inside my sleeve, coldly calculating my next target's trajectory.

"Shut up. Keep screaming and I’ll send you straight to your daughter."

The doctor beside me warned the howling man with a voice as cold as ice.

She had completely ripped off the mask of the gentle savior. It was the voice of an apex predator.

That was when it happened.

Beep— Beep— Beep— The rhythmic monitor suddenly flatlined into one long, horrific shriek. BEEEEEEEEEP—

A gruesome death sentence announcing that someone's heart had completely stopped.

Simultaneously, the pitch-black darkness trapping us shattered.

FLASH!

Blinding white light poured from the ceiling like a waterfall, stabbing our optic nerves like razor blades.

Through the agonizing pain of my retinas burning, my vision was forcibly restored, making me shed physiological tears.

Beyond the blurred haze, the full view of this hellish sealed ruin revealed itself.

And the moment my eyes locked onto the 'fourth survivor'—a figure curled up grotesquely in the corner, completely motionless—my heart, which I always kept under cold, perfect control, dropped for the very first time in my life.
