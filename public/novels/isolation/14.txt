## Episode 14: Rat in a Glass Box

The silver key glowed cherry red in my palm.

Along with the bizarre vibrations, an ominous mechanical whir began spewing from its microscopic seams. Then, with a heavy pop, the brilliant white surgical lights inside the bulletproof control room completely blacked out.

"No! Open it! Open the door right now!"

My husband's desperate scream erupted from the darkened room.

Red emergency lights flickered on, bathing the interior in a bloody hue, brutally exposing the pathetic sight of a beast trapped inside a perfectly sealed glass box.

What I was holding wasn't the master key to open this hunting ground.

It was the real detonator that would permanently seal his grave, dragging the arrogant man sitting in that control room into a living burial.

"Help me... Honey! I was wrong! Please, stop that device!"

The arrogant voice that had mocked me just moments ago was completely gone. Watching him smash his face against the thick bulletproof glass and beg for his life sent a thrill of pleasure down my spine so intense I forgot the agony in my torn arm.

With my face covered in blood, I walked right up to the glass and flashed him a chillingly bright smile.

But my grotesque euphoria froze instantly at the sound of a strange, highly unnatural noise echoing from the corner of the ruin.

"Heh... Hehehe... Hahahahaha!"

The grieving father, who had been holding his daughter's corpse and weeping.

The man who had crawled on the floor playing the weakest prey, was slowly straightening his spine and bursting into hysterical laughter.

He was no longer a father driven mad by grief.

With a callous push, he shoved Eun-ji's corpse aside and walked toward me with the perfectly controlled stride of an apex predator. He whispered quietly.

"It took exactly three years for that stupid bastard to dig his own grave. Don't you think, Madam CEO?"

The true architect, someone who had even used my husband as a pawn, had finally appeared.

And the moment I saw the real master key clutched in the man's hand, the rational calculating circuits in my brain completely shut down for the very first time in my life.
