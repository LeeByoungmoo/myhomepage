# PRESSURE
## Episode 14: The Table

---

The wail of sirens ripped through the city's night sky. 

An APB was issued across the entire metropolitan area. 'Armed collapse of the Federal Pressure Control Bureau and extraction of a highly classified asset (Level 8 child).' Prime suspect: Ian Han. 

Ian dropped the boy off at a safe location—near the Liberators' safehouse where Evelyn Thorne had found him—and headed straight back home. He was now a fugitive. Hundreds of elite Enforcers would be scouring the city, desperate to hunt him down. 

He needed to run. Smuggle himself out of the country, or maybe even join hands with The Liberators to wage war against this rotting government. That would be the 'logical' move for someone possessing Level 10 power. 

But Ian didn't stop walking. 
He made the most foolish, dangerous choice possible. He returned to the FEMA motel where his family was. 

*Click.* 

The front door opened. 
It was past midnight, and the only light in the living room was a single, dim desk lamp. 

Surprisingly, no one was asleep. They were all sitting in different corners of the room, as if they knew Ian was coming back, or perhaps they had seen the horrific news and were too terrified to sleep. 

Sarah sat on the sofa, her face buried in her hands. David was staring blankly out the window. Emma was curled up on a dining chair, nervously biting her fingernails, and only little Sam was fast asleep on the rug. 

As Ian walked in, every eye in the room snapped to him. 

"Ian..."
Sarah called out to him, her voice trembling. "The news... are those things they're saying... true? Did you really attack the Bureau..."

"Yes." 

Ian answered shortly. He didn't offer any excuses. 

David turned away from the window and let out a heavy sigh. 
"The Enforcers will be here any minute. You should have run."

"I know. But before I run, there's something we need to settle."

Ian took off his jacket, tossed it aside, and walked toward the small dining table. He pulled out the chair opposite Emma and sat down. 

"Everyone, come sit down."

There was absolutely no trace of Pressure or intimidation in Ian's voice. But that calm tone ironically created an inescapable weight. 

David and Sarah hesitated before slowly approaching the table. Sam, having been woken up, rubbed his eyes, grabbed the hem of Sarah's skirt, and toddled after her. 

The family of five sat around the cheap plastic table of the temporary motel room. 
Just a month ago, a family dinner was essentially a warzone. They all had to hold their breath, terrified of when Emma's Pressure might explode. And recently, it was Ian's unspoken Pressure that had been suffocating the entire family. 

"I saw the true face of the Bureau today."
Ian broke the silence. 

"They were completely destroying children's minds just to turn their Pressure into weapons. I also met The Liberators. They argued that the very institution of 'Family' is evil and needs to be abolished."

Ian slowly scanned the faces of his family. 
His father's resignation, his mother's fear, his sister's confusion. 

"They might be right. Pressure drives people insane, and our family is already broken beyond repair. But..."

Ian clenched his fists. 

"I decided I'm not going to run away. And I'm not going to become a king who crushes everything with power, either. Instead, I want us to start over. Right here. Without the lies, without the fear, and without the hierarchy of Pressure. Just... as a family."

Before Ian even finished speaking, a half-asleep Sam began to stumble around the table. The toddler didn't seem to realize how late it was, or how incredibly heavy the air in the room felt. 

Sam stopped right next to Emma's chair. 

Emma's shoulders flinched and went rigid. The trauma of the past—when Sam had accidentally spilled soup and she had lost control, nearly destroying the house—flashed vividly in her mind. Emma instinctively tried to pull back. 

But Sam just whined softly and climbed up onto Emma's lap. 

"Emmaaa..."

Emma froze solid. Her heart pounded like it was going to explode. Terrified that her Pressure might slip out of her control and hurt her little brother, she couldn't even breathe. 

Sarah gasped in horror and reached out to pull Sam away. 

"Leave him." 
Ian stopped Sarah. 

Sam settled comfortably onto Emma's lap and took her cold, stiff hands in his own two tiny hands. The child's warmth seeped into Emma's fingertips. 

"You're warm, Emma..."
Sam mumbled, burying his face in her stomach. 

Fat tears began to fall from Emma's eyes, *plop, plop*. 
The twelve-year-old girl who had been called a monster carefully... like a person terrified of breaking fragile glass... wrapped her arms around Sam's tiny back. 

There was no storm of Pressure. No destructive roar. 
It was just a sister hugging her little brother. A perfectly ordinary, flawless embrace. 

Everyone at the table held their breath, watching the scene unfold. 
It was a miracle. A miracle of pure, unadulterated trust—something that even Level 10 power could never create. 

"Now..." 
Ian said, his voice trembling slightly. 

"It's time to be honest with each other. If we really want to be a family."

2:00 AM. 
The long, agonizing confessions of the five people gathered around the table were about to begin.
