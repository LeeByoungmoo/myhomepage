# PRESSURE
## Episode 13: The Bureau

---

From the outside, the headquarters of the Federal Pressure Control Bureau looked like just another massive, boring government building. But as Ian followed Director Vance into a private elevator and descended to the fifth basement level, an entirely different world revealed itself. 

Cold metal corridors, heavy security, and a sterile, vacuum-sealed space that buzzed constantly with the hum of heavy-duty air filtration systems. 

"This is the beating heart of our Bureau." 
Director Vance spread his arms proudly. 

"In order for low-level, incompetent civilians to live their lives in peace, this is where we safely 'protect' out-of-control high-level users and train them to become valuable assets for the nation."

Ian walked down the corridor in silence. Lining both sides of the hallway were rooms made of thick, specialized glass. Next to each door was a medical chart displaying a 'Subject ID' and a 'Pressure Level.' 

Ian stopped and looked into the first room. 
An eight-year-old girl was sitting on the floor of an empty, blindingly white room, staring blankly at the wall. There was no furniture, no toys. The only thing in the room was the heavy padding of Pressure-absorbing panels lining the walls. 

"Why is that kid locked up in there?" Ian asked. 
"She isn't locked up. She's in isolation training." Vance flipped a page on his clipboard. "Level 7. A case where the parents couldn't handle the child's Pressure and gave up on raising her. If we leave a kid like that alone, she's a potential terrorist. So, the government steps in as the parent to guide her in the right direction."

"The right direction?"
"We teach them how to follow orders and how to use their power for the good of the country."

Ian clenched his fists. The Bureau might not be trying to dismantle the family unit like The Liberators, but at the end of the day, this was just state-sanctioned kidnapping and child abuse. 

They stopped at the end of the corridor, in front of a heavily guarded door marked 'Maximum Containment.' 

Director Vance scanned his iris, and the massive steel door hissed open. 
Inside, a six-year-old boy, barely two years older than Ian's little brother Sam, was crouched in the corner. The chart on the wall read: [Level 8]. 

"Our greatest masterpiece." Vance smiled. "His parents died in an accident. He was sent to a foster home, where he had a total meltdown. By the time we brought him in, he was practically a feral animal... but now, he is an outstanding 'Enforcer Cadet.'"

Ian stepped closer to the glass. 
Sensing a presence, the boy slowly lifted his head. His eyes were dull and lifeless. The fluctuations in his Pressure were completely flat, as if his emotions had been entirely castrated. 

Looking at Ian through the glass, the boy spoke in a dry, mechanical voice. 

"Are you my family, mister?"

Ian's heart plummeted. 
He thought of Emma, who had awakened at three years old. A little sister who became an object of terror to her own parents because of an uncontrollable power. If people like Agent Osborne had dragged Emma down into this pit, she would have been stripped of her humanity and raised as a federal weapon just like this kid. 

When the family was destroyed, a more massive, far more violent master named the Government just stepped in to take its place. 

"Director Vance." 
Ian called out, his voice sinking ice-cold. 

"Yes, Ian? Are the facilities to your liking?"

"You people are even worse scum than The Liberators."

Vance's face hardened. 
"Ian, you're crossing a line. We do this to maintain order..."

*Vwoooom—!*

Before Vance could even finish his sentence, an insane vibration tore through the entire fifth basement level. Sirens shrieked, piercing their eardrums, and emergency red strobe lights began flashing from the ceiling. 

"What... what the hell is happening!" Vance yelled into his radio. 

"Director! The Pressure readings... Ian's Pressure readings are spiking! They're off the charts!"

Ian placed his hand flat against the glass wall. The specialized reinforced glass, designed to withstand a Level 8 rampage, began to spiderweb with a sickening *crack*. 

"Ian! Stop this instantly! Do you have any idea where you are? If you destroy a federal facility, you will be classified as a domestic terrorist!" 
Vance flared his Level 6 Pressure and reached out to grab Ian's shoulder. 

But Ian just casually turned his head and glared at him. Without Ian lifting a finger, Vance was blasted fifteen feet down the corridor, as if he'd been hit by an invisible sledgehammer. 

*CRASH—!!*

The reinforced glass of the Maximum Containment room shattered into a million pieces. 
The six-year-old boy stared at Ian with wide, startled eyes. 

Ian brushed the dust off his jacket and held out a hand to the kid. 
"I'm not your family, but I can get you out of here. Wanna come with me?"

As if possessed, the little boy reached out and took Ian's hand. 
At that exact moment, dozens of tactical agents flooded into the corridor, weapons drawn. They were all elite Enforcers, Level 5 and above. 

"Don't move!"

Ian stepped in front of the little boy, hiding him behind his back. 
A cold smile spread across Ian's face. The fear that had always forced him to suppress his power was completely gone. For the first time, he realized exactly what this terrifying Level 10 power was meant for. 

"If this is the order you built."

A tidal wave of Pressure exploded upward from beneath Ian's feet. 

"I'll just have to break it all down."

On that day, the underground facility of the Federal Pressure Control Bureau—an impenetrable fortress—was completely leveled by a single sixteen-year-old boy. 

It was the moment the monster who wore the crown, Ian, willingly chose to become an enemy of the state.
