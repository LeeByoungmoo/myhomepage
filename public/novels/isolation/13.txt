## Episode 13: Silver Betrayal

The cold tip of the knife plunged mercilessly into the boy's red scar.

A beast-like scream bubbled up from his gagged throat. Hot, sticky blood gushed out, dying the back of my hand red, but my heartbeat remained chillingly calm.

The sloppy stitches snapped one by one under the blunt force of the blade.

I shoved my blunt fingers deep into the boy's blood-slicked incision.

"Mmph... Mmmph!"

His body convulsed violently in ultimate agony, but I callously rummaged through his abdomen. Soon, among the soft, pulsing organs, my fingertips caught on a small, hard metal capsule.

Shhhk.

I violently yanked out the blood-soaked capsule and slowly stood up. At my feet, the boy rolled his eyes back, gasping for breath as he slowly lost consciousness.

Twisting the capsule open, a beautifully crafted, heavy silver key dropped onto the floor.

The only master key capable of accessing the bulletproof control room from this hellish hunting ground. I picked up the bloody key and slowly turned my head toward the glass where my husband sat.

But the scene inside the control room was playing out completely differently than I expected.

My husband's face—previously sitting arrogantly in his chair, treating this slaughter as pure entertainment—was contorted in extreme bewilderment and sheer terror.

With trembling hands, he frantically smashed the microphone button on his console.

"You... What the hell did you just do! The thing in that capsule isn't a door key—!"

His desperate, cracking scream tore through the speakers.

And in that exact moment, a faint metallic whine emanated from the seams of the silver key in my hand, and the entire object began to vibrate violently while emitting a glaring red light.

It wasn't a simple master key to open a door.

It was the real detonator—a red, glowing trigger that even the architect husband didn't know existed, or had entirely lost control over.
