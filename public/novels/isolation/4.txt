# PRESSURE
## Episode 4: What Fathers Carry

---

The family moved into a temporary residence in just two days. 

It was a cramped, depressing motel room assigned by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) for 'Disaster Victims.' Officially, the incident Emma caused was recorded as an 'unexplained localized barometric anomaly.' Agent Osborne had obviously pulled strings behind the scenes. The feds didn't want the public branding a Level 8 user as a domestic terrorist. 

Amidst the duffel bags and cardboard boxes scattered across the cheap motel carpet, David sat crouched and alone. 

The rest of the family had collapsed from exhaustion. Emma hadn't spoken a single word during the entire move. Ian, sporting a butterfly bandage on his forehead, had silently hauled bags back and forth from the minivan. Sarah paced nervously in front of the mini-fridge before finally taking Sam into her arms and closing her eyes on the lumpy mattress. 

David looked down at his own two hands. 
Thick-knuckled and calloused. Once, these hands were strong enough to protect anything. 

*If only... if only I still had a little more power.*

David’s memory drifted back nine years, to the days when he was still an elite Level 6 Enforcer. 

***

*Nine years ago, Summer.*

That day, David had just finished subduing an out-of-control Level 5 user and was heading home from work. As he pulled into the driveway of his suburban home, he noticed a shift in the air currents. 

A suffocating, highly dense Pressure. 
It was billowing out from his own front lawn. 

"Sarah!"

David broke into a sprint instinctively. The scene in his front yard was a nightmare. Sarah’s minivan was floating mid-air, and the concrete driveway beneath it was fractured like a spider web. 

And in the center of it all was Emma, who had just turned three. 

"Kyahaha!"

Emma was giggling brightly at the floating car. The toddler seemed to think it was magic, or some fun new game. But Sarah, who had narrowly avoided being crushed under the vehicle, was slumped on the lawn, clutching her scraped knee, her face drained of all color. 

"Emma, no! Put it down!"

David yelled as he ran toward them. He released his Level 6 Pressure, slamming it directly against Emma’s force. 

*CRASH!*

The collision of the two invisible forces generated a massive shockwave. The hovering minivan plummeted with a deafening screech of metal against concrete. 

"Waaaaah!" 

Emma burst into tears. She was angry that her 'toy' was broken, and startled that her father had hurt her. The uncontrollable rage of a three-year-old morphed into a Pressure storm that swept over David once again. 

In that moment, David realized the terrifying truth. 
*This child is stronger than my Level 6. She's at least a Level 7, maybe a Level 8.*

David was a highly trained Enforcer. There was only one way to neutralize a rampaging user: shatter their Pressure completely and force them into submission. 

David reached out toward Emma. He channeled every ounce of his strength to grab the child by the collar and pin her to the ground. It was the only way to protect his wife and stop the rampage. 

But right before his hand touched Emma's small neck...
He looked into her eyes. 

*Daddy, what did I do wrong?*

The innocent, confused eyes of a pitiful child who, through no fault of her own, was born with far too much power. 

David's hand froze in mid-air. 
If he used his power to suppress Emma now? This child would learn that 'force' is the only absolute way to control the world. The second a father beat his daughter into submission with power, their family was dead. 

David withdrew his Pressure. 
And he took Emma's storm head-on with his bare body. 

That day, David suffered three broken ribs and a ruptured right eardrum. 
But the real tragedy began after that. 

Following the incident, David secretly dug through the Bureau's classified archives. He found records of illegal and highly dangerous human experiments regarding 'Pressure Suppression.' 

*If I use my power to suppress her every time Emma loses control, we won't be a family. We'll be a warden and a prisoner.*

He made his choice. To prevent himself from becoming a monster, he decided to pull out his own fangs. 

***

*Present.*

David let out a ragged breath, snapping back to reality. 
After the black-market procedure that night, his Level plummeted from a 6 to a 2. He was diagnosed with permanent Pressure damage and took an early medical retirement from the Enforcers. 

He had chosen to become weak for the sake of his family. He believed that if the father gave up his power, the daughter could escape the cycle of dominance and learn what normal love felt like. 

But the result was catastrophic. 
When the father lost his power, the unchecked daughter became the undisputed tyrant of the household. His noble sacrifice hadn't saved his family; it had only created the perfect breeding ground for a terrifying monster. 

"I... I ruined everything."

David buried his face in his hands. The heavy air of regret pressed down on his shaking shoulders in the dark motel room. 

There was nothing he could do now. 
At least, that was what he thought. 

But at that exact moment, in the cramped bathroom just beyond the thin motel wall... 
His eldest son, Ian, was drenched in a cold sweat, battling an unfamiliar sensation called 'power' for the very first time in his sixteen years of life.
