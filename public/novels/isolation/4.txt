## Episode 4: The Second Predator

That cold whisper pierced my ear.

Instantly, the pathetic sobs leaking from my lips stopped dead. In a world stripped of sight, the calm, rhythmic breathing of the woman holding me became the only reality.

Her breath carried a faint but deeply familiar scent.

The smell of cold surgical steel and heavy antiseptics. This woman, who had approached me playing the role of a sweet, ordinary savior, was wearing a perfectly calculated mask of her own.

Darkness is an excellent shield for hypocrisy.

I slowly uncurled my spine. Gripping the hunting knife hidden in my sleeve, I pressed the razor-sharp tip flush against her abdomen, right through her thin shirt.

"The waistband takes one second longer to draw."

I erased the fragile, terrified voice I’d been using and whispered back with a chilling, bone-dry tone. As the blade pressed into her abdominal muscles, I felt her breath hitch ever so slightly.

"Fascinating. You're much better than I expected."

She didn’t panic at the pressure of the blade. Instead, she let out a low, eerie chuckle that tickled the shell of my ear. The realization that there was another psycho in this hellhole sent an electric thrill down my spine.

"Hey! What are you two doing in the corner?!"

The man I had crippled earlier shouted nervously. To the two men blinded by the dark, it probably sounded like the doctor and I were just huddling together in terror.

"It's nothing. She's just having a hard time calming down..."

Even with my knife a fraction of an inch away from puncturing her skin, the doctor spun a flawless, gentle lie into the air. Without loosening my grip on the handle, I played along, letting out a series of wheezy, trembling gasps to match her horrific acting.

Right then.

The massive stone floor beneath our feet began to tilt with a sickening, grinding noise.

This wasn't the passing rumble of a subway.

It was the deafening roar of massive gears grinding together, tilting the entire underground ruin. Rough stone dust rained down as the floor steeply slanted, pulling terrified screams from the two men.

When the grinding stopped, the heavy pressure in our eardrums popped, and a horrific noise slashed through the silence.

*Beep— Beep— Beep—*

A crisp, rhythmic electronic sound echoing in the dark. It was the exact sound of a cardiac monitor—the same noise that rang endlessly in the hospital wards where I used to deny surgical insurance payouts with a single stroke of my pen.
