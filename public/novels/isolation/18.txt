# PRESSURE
## Episode 18: Convergence

---

The city was burning. 

Ian led the injured Emma and his parents to an abandoned subway station on the outskirts of the city to hide. He laid Emma down on a dusty, moldy bench, and Sarah wept as she applied pressure to her daughter's wounds. 

But the deafening roars echoing from the surface above didn't let them rest. 

*BOOOOOOM—!*

A massive explosion shook the city skyline in the distance. The shockwave of Pressure traveled all the way down to the subway station, shattering the fluorescent lights overhead. 

"What's going on...?"
Ian ran up the concrete stairs and looked toward the downtown skyline. 

The night sky was painted with the blue glow of raw Pressure energy and raging fire. The epicenter of the explosions was the Federal Pressure Control Bureau headquarters. 

*The Liberators.*

Ian knew it instantly. Evelyn Thorne's extremist group, 'The Liberators,' had seized the opportunity created when Ian destroyed the Maximum Containment floor and launched a full-scale assault on the Bureau. 

A massive war between those who wanted to dismantle the 'Family' prison, and those who wanted to control power and turn people into federal assets. And right in the middle of that warzone was his father. 

"Ian."
Sarah grabbed Ian's sleeve from the bottom of the stairs. Her face was covered in tears and dirt. 

"Your dad... are you going to get him?"

His mother's voice was shaking. Nine years ago, she had been furious to learn her husband had deceived her and chosen to live as an incompetent man, but right now, her eyes held nothing but the pure, unadulterated terror of losing him. 

"Yeah."
Ian nodded. 

"Take me with you."
Emma said, limping up behind them. Every breath she took made her grimace in pain from her broken ribs, but her eyes were burning like a feral beast. 

"I can fight too. With my Pressure... I can save Dad..."

"No." 
Ian cut her off firmly. 

"This isn't a fight you can win just by having a bigger number. If you go out there, you'll just add fuel to the fire. Stay here and protect Mom and Sam. That's your job."

Ian roughly, but affectionately, ruffled Emma's hair. 
"I'll protect this family and bring him back."

Ian turned around and started sprinting toward the burning center of the city. 

***

The plaza in front of the Bureau headquarters was absolute chaos. 
Over a hundred high-level users belonging to The Liberators were unleashing their Pressure, engaged in brutal hand-to-hand combat with the Enforcers. The asphalt was cratered, and cars were crumpled like tissue paper, being thrown through the air. 

"Tear down this rotting prison!!"
Evelyn Thorne screamed from atop a pile of rubble, unleashing her Level 8 Pressure in all directions. Enforcers who were caught in her blast radius screamed and collapsed. 

But the Bureau's counterattack was just as fierce. 
Elite agents led by Director Vance were pushing The Liberators back using tactical vehicles and heavy-duty Pressure-suppression arrays. 

And right in the middle of that battlefield. 
David lay face down, covered in blood, at Director Vance's feet. 

"You're a stubborn son of a bitch."
Director Vance said coldly, grinding the heel of his dress shoe into David's crushed hand. 

"A mere Level 6, taking out seven elite Enforcers to buy time for an escape? That's some admirable fatherly love. But it looks like your son abandoned you to save his own skin."

David coughed up blood, but let out a mocking chuckle. 

"My son doesn't run away like me... he's not garbage like you."

"You talk too much. Let me show you how traitors meet their end."
Vance pressed the barrel of his tactical rifle against the back of David's head. 

Right at that moment. 
Every single sound on the battlefield was instantly erased. 

The fighting Liberators and Enforcers all stopped moving, as if they had planned it. They couldn't breathe. The air turned thick like syrup, and a colossal, crushing weight began to press down on their shoulders. 

Both Evelyn Thorne and Director Vance turned their heads. 

At the entrance to the plaza, where the smoke and flames had parted. 
A single boy in a high school t-shirt and jeans was walking toward them. 

"Ian Han..."
Vance's jaw trembled uncontrollably. 

Ian didn't look angry. He wasn't projecting any murderous intent. Just the sheer fact that he existed in that space was turning everyone on the battlefield into insignificant specks of dust. 

It was the arrival of the unprecedented, theoretical absolute: Level 10.
