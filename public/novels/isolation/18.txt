## Episode 18: The Burning Abyss

My vision flashed white from the warmth of the sticky blood slapping my cheek.

The scarred man's head snapped back at a brutal angle, and dark red blood erupted like a fountain from the exact center of his cross-shaped scar.

The bullet from my son's gun flawlessly pierced the man's windpipe with zero margin of error.

As the massive body crashed down like a rotting tree, the red master key clattered across the floor and rolled right to the metal foot of my son's prosthetic leg.

"I almost burned to death twice, but I guess I shot first this time."

My son walked slowly toward me, the rough scraping of his metal leg echoing loudly.

Even the horrific screams of my husband melting in the control room had entirely stopped. Now, only the heavy, suffocating scent of blood and gunpowder weighed down the air in the ruin.

"You've aged a lot, Mother."

The cold barrel shifted direction and pressed precisely against the center of my forehead.

The fragile boy who had cried and begged for his life inside a car plunging off a cliff ten years ago was gone. He had returned as an apex predator with the eyes of a beast.

Facing the barrel, I slowly lifted my blood-soaked face.

Instead of dropping to my knees in terror and weeping with maternal guilt like a typical cliché, I let my mangled left arm hang loose and flashed a chillingly bloody smile.

"If I had known you were still alive, I wouldn't have bothered cutting the brakes. I should have strangled you with my own two hands."

At my cold venom, the tire-track scar around my son's right eye twitched ever so slightly.

Without a word, he bent down and picked up the red master key—our only survival device. And without a second thought, he tossed it straight down into the gaping vertical shaft reeking of gasoline.

"I suppose you should have. But now, it looks like Mother will have to go down there and fetch it herself."

Click. He pulled back the hammer of the gun pressed to my forehead.

In that instant, a massive pillar of fire erupted like a snake from the very bottom of the shaft where the key had fallen, burning the red image permanently into my retinas.
