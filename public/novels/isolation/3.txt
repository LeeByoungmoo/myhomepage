# PRESSURE
## Episode 3: Fracture

---

Dinner was always the most nerve-wracking time of the day. 

The entire family had to gather in one place, and no one dared to cross Emma. Tonight’s menu was roast beef and mashed potatoes—Emma’s favorites. Yet, the air over the dining table felt exceptionally heavy. 

Perhaps it was because of Agent Osborne’s visit earlier in the day. The Pressure swirling around Emma was violently unstable. The silverware on the table hummed and vibrated slightly, and the overhead light flickered at an erratic rhythm. 

David, their father, swallowed his food with a grim face. Sarah fed Sam his mac and cheese, her movements painfully slow, terrified of letting her spoon clink against the bowl. Ian held his breath and rushed through his meal. 

"Mom. Water."

Emma murmured. Startled, Sarah reached for the water pitcher. Right at that moment, Sam, playing with a toy dinosaur, swung his arm out. 

*Smack!* 

The little boy's hand collided with a steaming bowl of gravy. The gravy boat tipped over, and scalding brown liquid spilled directly onto Emma’s lap. 

"Ah, hot!"

Emma screamed and shot up from her chair. 
And in that instant, hell broke loose. 

*KABOOM—!* 

Like a bomb detonating in a closed room, a massive shockwave exploded outward from the dining table. It was a Level 8 Pressure eruption, completely out of control. 

"Kyaaa!" 

Sarah shrieked as the dining table split down the middle and launched into the air. The chairs smashed into the walls, shattering into splinters. The living room windows blew out simultaneously with a deafening roar. In the midst of the terrifying chaos and flying shrapnel, Sarah instinctively threw her body over Sam to shield him. 

David was blasted over the sofa. Once a Level 6 Enforcer, his current Level 2 body couldn't withstand his daughter's raw detonation. He groaned, lying on a bed of broken glass, trying to push himself up, but the overwhelming Pressure pinned him down so hard he couldn't even twitch a finger. 

"Aaaaaah!" 

Emma was clutching her wet jeans, screaming. It wasn't because of the burn. It was pure panic. The realization that she had lost control again, the terror that she was destroying something else, was swallowing her whole. The Pressure grew even more ferocious. The ceiling fan was seconds away from crashing down. 

Then it happened. 

"STOP IT!!!"

A roar ripped through the deafening noise. 
It was Ian. 

Blood was trickling down his forehead where a piece of the table had clipped him. Standing up straight in this tempest of Pressure seemed physically impossible, but he was crawling across the hardwood floor right up to Emma, screaming at the top of his lungs. 

"I said stop it! Cut this crazy bullshit out! He's four years old! He's a four-year-old kid!"

Sixteen years of repressed rage and sorrow erupted from Ian like a live volcano. He reached out to grab Emma by the shoulders, but an invisible wall of Pressure violently repelled him, sending him skidding backward. Still, he refused to give up. He forced himself up onto his knees and shouted. 

"How long are we supposed to live terrified of your moods?! We can't even groan when it hurts, we have to smile when we're pissed off! You call this a family?! You call this a home?!"

Dead silence. 
It wasn't a physical silence. The storm was still raging, but Ian's roar seemed to have swallowed every other sound in the house. 

Emma’s eyes widened to the size of saucers. 
Shock. Horror. And... unfamiliarity. 

Since the day she was born, no one had ever yelled at her like this. Her parents always pacified her or cowered in fear. Her brother always avoided her eyes. Agent Osborne treated her like a weapon. 

Ian was the first person to show her raw, unfiltered, human 'anger.' 

Like a magic trick, Emma’s Pressure died down. The debris floating in the air crashed to the floor. The only sounds left in the wrecked living room were Ian's ragged breathing and Sam's hysterical crying. 

"Ian..."

Emma murmured in a daze. Tears welled up in her eyes. It wasn't from fear. It was a strange sense of relief—the feeling that she wasn't a monster, but just a little kid being scolded by her older brother. 

But that peace was fleeting. 

"Emma! Are you okay?!" 

David limped over and wrapped his arms protectively around his daughter. The thick veil of terror toward his own child was still plastered on his face. Sarah, too, was clutching Sam in her trembling arms, casting terrified, wary glances at Emma. 

Emma looked into her parents' eyes and saw her own reflection. 
They still saw the uncontrollable monster. Ian’s outburst might have created a momentary crack, but the fortress of fear built over nine years wasn't going to crumble that easily. 

"Don't touch me!"

Emma shoved David away violently. It was a physical force a Level 2 couldn't handle. David tumbled helplessly backward onto the floor. 

"Stay away from me! I hate looking at all of you!"

Emma wailed and fled to her room, slamming the door so hard it nearly splintered off its hinges. 

In the middle of the ruined living room, Ian sat slumped on the floor, wiping the blood from his forehead. He gripped his trembling knees. 

He had finally spoken up. But the situation had only gotten worse. The house was physically wrecked, and the pathetic shell they called a family was completely torn to shreds. 

This was the limit of the powerless. 

Ian looked out through the shattered living room windows, staring up at the glass dome of the Top-Tier academy glowing in the distance. Blood mixed with tears streamed down his cheeks. 

*I can't do this anymore.*

Deep within Ian’s core, something cold and hard began to curdle. 

*I won't live like this anymore.*

That night, Ian suffered from a horrifying migraine. He heard a tearing sound, like something rupturing inside his brain, and every blood vessel in his body throbbed like it was going to burst. 

And as he passed out, drenched in cold sweat, 
the desk, the chair, and the bed in his room... 

silently levitated three feet into the air.
