## Episode 3: The Cold Fangs in the Sleeve

The cold, smooth metal pressed into my palm. The heavy, balanced weight of a perfectly crafted hunting knife sharpened my senses.

Someone had deliberately thrown a weapon into this darkness. I could practically smell the thick, bloody malice of a mastermind hoping we would tear each other apart like starved beasts.

Without a sound, I slipped the knife deep inside the sleeve of my thin cardigan. As the cold steel kissed my bare skin, a thrilling sense of control sent shivers down my spine.

"Nobody move! If you come any closer, I'll kill you all!"

The man whose arms I had just crippled howled like an animal. The realization that the owner of the 'scar' from his shattered memories was trapped in here with him had driven him half-mad.

"Stop it! Do you want us all to die?!"

The other woman, trying to mediate, snapped into the dark. Then, I heard her cautious footsteps approaching me.

Her hands gently wrapped around my trembling shoulders. Gripping the knife handle inside my sleeve in a reverse grip, I squeezed out a pitiful, breathless voice.

"Sob... I'm so scared. That man suddenly tried to kill me..."

Taking the bait of my fake tears, she slowly patted my back, matching my breathing with gentle sympathy.

"It's okay. I'm right here. I won't let that maniac near you."

I smiled into the pitch-black void, thrilled to have secured a perfect human shield.

But then, my smile vanished.

Through the thin fabric of my cardigan, I felt her fingers. They were freakishly long and cold.

In total darkness, your sense of touch goes into overdrive. There were tiny, hard calluses on the tips of her index and middle fingers. The hands of a surgeon who held cold scalpels for hours, or someone very used to handling precise, deadly tools.

She must have felt my shoulder muscles instinctively lock up, because her hands stopped moving.

Then, slipping through her lips and brushing against my ear, a chillingly dry whisper slithered in—so low the two men couldn't possibly hear.

"A knife is much easier to draw from your waistband than your sleeve. Don't you agree, Madam CEO?"
